The family chore game

Chore Royale: Spin the wheel. Earn Crowns. Take the throne.

Chore Royale turns the family into a royal court. Every player on their own phone grabs chores, plays power cards and races for the weekly crown. A parent referees with a photo check.

The problem

“In a minute” means never.

Chores turn into a nightly negotiation. Parents nag, kids stall, and nobody keeps score fairly.

Kids already love games with ranks, cards and a leaderboard. So the court asked: what if the chores were the game?

Let the wheel decide.
In a minute… Not my turn! I did it yesterday!
How it plays

Four moves. Zero arguments.

The game keeps score. A parent only referees, and points land only after a parent says yes.

I

Grab a chore

Every chore shows its points. Big ones glow. Up to three at once, first come first served.

II

Steal, swap, duel

Play a Steal card, block with a Shield, offer a swap with bonus points, or challenge a sibling to a week-long duel.

III

Snap the proof

Tap done and send a photo. The parent approves on their phone and the points land.

IV

Win the crown

The weekly leaderboard resets every Sunday. The top player gets the crown ceremony.

The game keeps score

Nobody, kid or parent, can type in points. Parents only approve or send a chore back.

Little ones can win

Duels count chores done, not reflexes, so a younger kid can beat an older one.

Kids’ data stays home

No email or phone. A parent approves every new phone, and proof photos are deleted after review.

The front door

First screen: grab chores, score points.

The game opens straight into a three-step welcome, on desktop and on the phone. No store page, no sign-up wall.

Design system

A knight with a spoon.

The crowned helm holds the Spoon-Sceptre: half royal, half household. Gold type with a Royal Ink outline, like a game title screen.
