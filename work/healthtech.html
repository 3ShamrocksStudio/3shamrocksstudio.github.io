Role

Two companies. Two clear remits.

App and brand lead

VIBRANT

A vibrating capsule for chronic constipation. I led its companion app and its brand. The VIBRANT capsule was named one of TIME’s Best Inventions of 2023.

Brand and product marketing lead

AposHealth

A clinical treatment for knee and back pain, built on biomechanical footwear. I led the brand and the product marketing.

I did not build a product at AposHealth. My remit was the brand and how the product was marketed.

Both were in-house roles, as an employee. This is not client work.

What I did

Made medicine feel simple, without losing the science.

Health products have to earn trust twice: from the patient who uses them and from the clinician who recommends them.

Turned clinical detail into clear pictures

Treatment schedules, body mechanics and joint pain, shown so a patient understands them at a glance.

Built one brand for every touchpoint

Logo rules, colour, icons and illustration, applied the same way on the website, in the app and in marketing.

Designed for patients and clinicians

Warm and human for the people being treated. Precise and credible for the people treating them.
