A2How it works

Raw jam in. Post out.

Three taps, real screens. It all happens on the phone, in seconds.

  1. Drop in your jams

    Record, or add a whole gallery. Each jam gets a stamp: Keep, Review or Trash.

  2. Get your lick

    The best complete phrase, starting and ending on the bar, plus two runners-up. Nudge it a bar either way.

  3. Make it a stage

    Eight Looks, a title and captions. Straight to Reels, TikTok or Shorts.
