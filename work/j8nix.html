What it is

Many projects. One answer: what needs me now?

A studio running a dozen products loses track of who is blocked on what. J8NIX started on 15 June 2026 as a “smart operations OS” and, three days later, was rebuilt into the studio’s command centre.

  • Waiting on youA priority list of the exact decisions and reviews each project is waiting for.
  • Every project, one cardA status pill, the next step, and a button that opens the real build.
  • Honest about its limitsThe Message Claude box copies a ready command. A live AI link needs a backend, so it is marked “future phase”.
