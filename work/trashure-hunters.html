The Cosmic VoidRealm 1 of 6 · the problem

No villains. Only neglect.

Kids see litter every day and learn to walk past it. In the game, five young Elemonsters grew up across five realms. When people in our world stopped caring, litter drifted into the River of Three and the light dimmed. The only thing that pushes it back is real care, in the real world.
