The Cosmic VoidRealm 1 of 6 · the problem

No villains. Only neglect.

Kids see litter every day and learn to walk past it. In the game, five young Elemonsters grew up across five realms. When people in our world stopped caring, litter drifted into the River of Three and the light dimmed. The only thing that pushes it back is real care, in the real world.

GameThe light comes back
King GrimeNot evil, just what forgotten trash becomes. Every cleanup shrinks him.

Who it’s for

Kids 8–14

Your own hero quest.

  • Hatch an Elemonster and grow it with every cleanup
  • Earn XP and Trash-Cash for real litter picked up
  • Hunt Trashures in AR with your Water Gun
Parents

Screen time that ends outside.

  • Made for a grown-up and a child to play side by side
  • Under-13s need your yes before camera or location turn on
  • No chat, no ads to kids, no loot boxes, no pay-to-win
Schools and partners

A cleanup they ask for.

  • Class and team challenges for eco-clubs and Earth Day
  • Avatar-only teammates, so it fits supervised settings
  • Looking for first pilot schools, sponsors, cities and NGOs

The treasureWhat a cleanup pays out

Trash in. Treasure out.

Every verified cleanup pays in the game’s own currencies, and all of it is earned by cleaning, never bought.

Trash-Cash

The game coin. Spent in the store on Water Gun upgrades.

XP

Grows your Elemonster from Egg to Baby, Teen and Guardian, and opens the next realm.

Zones captured

Clean enough and the Grime Zone turns your team’s colour.

Compos to free

Trapped inside Grime creatures. Free them in Trashure-Hunt and they power up your Water Gun. Each one is a little Trashure of its own.

Light of the Temple

Every cleanup sends light back to Orbo at the Temple of Light.

The crowned form

The final stage. Each Elemonster earns its crown as Realm Keeper.

Cleanup verified

Trash-Cam checks the before and after for trash, not people, before anything pays out.
