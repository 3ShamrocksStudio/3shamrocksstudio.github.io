How it works · concept

Three moves to a real intro.

01

Send a challenge

Share your own link in a LinkedIn message, email or WhatsApp. The other side plays in the browser, no account.

02

Play ten minutes

Five minutes on each clock. Something to do together that isn't a pitch, with a natural end.

03

The Handshake

Good game. Both sides can choose to swap a one-line intro and connect.

The Lounge, for events and communities

Guests scan a QR code, get paired by level and play on their phones. A big screen shows the live leaderboard and the King of the Lounge.

Key moment

At the board, a rookie can reach a king.

A junior founder can play a CEO as an equal for ten minutes. Push the pawn, tap a highlighted square or use the button, and watch the line connect.

Design system · brand v7

One piece, two players.

The mark is one chess piece split by a single line: the Rookie in Electric Blue and the King in white. The wordmark turns the OO of ROOKIE into an infinity loop, the network that keeps going after the game. Navy, blue and white lead. Cool steel carries the cold and the waiting; warm amber is kept for the win: the handshake, the connection.
