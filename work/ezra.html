01The problem

Small print, long menus, one wrong tap.

Refills, bills, rides and official letters keep moving into apps and phone menus. For many older adults each one is a small obstacle, and a scam call can look just like a real one.

EZRA's idea is simple: say what you need, and EZRA does the steps.

Find the refill form, log in, choose a branch
Read a long letter in small print
Book a ride through three screens
One button instead of every step above. Tap it.
02What I designed

Three calm steps.

The whole product, reduced to one rhythm: ask, let EZRA do it, see that it is done.

1

Tap one big button

Ten everyday tasks on one screen. Large type, large targets, no menus.

2

EZRA takes it from there

EZRA asks only what it needs, then does the steps and shows what it did.

3

Check before you trust

Scam check is on the home screen, not hidden in settings.

04Design system

One stem, read both ways.

The Hebrew ע and the Latin E share one upright stem. Hebrew reads right to left, English left to right. Steady blue for trust, coral for warmth.
