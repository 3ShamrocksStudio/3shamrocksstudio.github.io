What I built

From a PDF table to a message you can send.

Real data, cited

CBS paid-rent averages for 18 cities, 6 districts and 4 room bands, updated for new leases. The source sits next to every number.

A verdict you feel

A fairness dial, the gap in shekels per year, and bars against the city, district and national averages.

The next move

A Hebrew negotiation message in two tones, with the CBS figure inside. Copy it or open it in WhatsApp.

Identity

Night city, one fair green.
