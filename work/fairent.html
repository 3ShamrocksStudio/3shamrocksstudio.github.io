Stop paying more for less.

Is your rent fair? Know before you sign.

FaiRent is a mobile app for renters in Israel. It compares an asking rent with what renters in that city actually pay, from official CBS survey data, then writes a polite negotiation message with the source inside.

The problem

Renters negotiate blind. The data exists.

Listing sites show asking prices. The CBS publishes what people actually pay, but in PDF tables nobody reads before signing.
