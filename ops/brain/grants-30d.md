## Top 3 to act on (15 Nov 2026)

1. **Google for Startups Cloud Program, Start tier ($2,000 credits, rolling).** This is the fastest real money: under an hour to apply, with decisions in about 5 to 7 days per the page, and it offsets the Firebase/GCP bill SH✡MER already runs on. **NEEDS YOUR EYES** on three eligibility risks. The company must be "founded within the past 2 years". The page excludes "dev shops, consultancies or agencies", so apply as the SH✡MER product and not as the design studio. The business email domain must match a live company website. A Google Cloud billing account ID is required; whether a card is needed is not stated on the page.
2. **EIC Accelerator 2027, Step 1 short proposal for SH✡MER (free to submit).** The official EIC Work Programme 2027 lists full-proposal cut-offs of **20 Jan 2027** and **17 Mar 2027**. A short proposal has to be accepted before a full one, so 20 Jan is very tight and **17 Mar 2027 is the realistic target**. Two things make it worth one 2 to 3 day effort: evaluator feedback, and reusable text for Tnufa and Horizon CL3 2027. The odds are low (about 1%) unless the pitch has a real technical core. The EIC runs an online "EIC Accelerator Challenges" info day on **20 Nov 2026**.
3. **Cloudflare for Startups, Tier 3 ($10,000 credits, rolling, bootstrapped OK).** This is the largest no-investor credit confirmed on a primary page this pass, reviewed in about 48 hours. It is useful only for the parts of the stack that can run on Cloudflare (landing pages, Workers, R2). **NEEDS YOUR EYES**: it **requires a valid payment method on file**, overages are billed automatically, and the card is charged when credits expire. The page also excludes "consultancies, agencies" and asks for a business email on the company's own domain, so apply as the SH✡MER product, not the studio.

Also worth doing (no action needed from Dave): the IIA **local-authority pilots track** is the best-fit **cash** route for SH✡MER Command, but its page was blocked again. The next pass should read it with a real browser.
