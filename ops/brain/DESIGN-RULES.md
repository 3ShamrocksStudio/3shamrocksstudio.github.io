# Design rules — iron-clad (Dave, 22 Nov 2026)
Bind every design, UX/UI, layout and brand task for J8NIX and every 3Shamrocks brand or product, past and future.

1. Every new design begins with deep, insightful research.
2. Forever: no "Claude-based" design systems, defaults or look-alike patterns. Always start fresh from research and from Dave's inputs.
3. When needed, research reference designs and proven best-practice layouts, concepts and UX/UI patterns, for the best user experience and deep brand-identity integration.
4. Originality is key at 3Shamrocks Studio. Every existing design that leans on Claude-default patterns must be brought under these rules.
5. Keep improving: design understanding, the meaning behind concepts, and the full design and branding process.
6. If unsure, ask Dave before designing.

Process for any design task: (a) research brief with sources and references → (b) Dave's inputs (references he likes, goals) → (c) concept directions → (d) build → (e) review against these rules.
