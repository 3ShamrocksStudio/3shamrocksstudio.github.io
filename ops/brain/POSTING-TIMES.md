# Posting times: standing rule (Dave, 14 Nov 2026)

Every social post goes out at the best time for **that account's own audience**, based on data, with the format that gets the most reach. Never post just because the post is ready. Re-check this file every month and whenever a platform's own analytics give real numbers for our accounts (our own numbers beat studies).

## Sources (fetched 14 Nov 2026)
- Buffer, 4.8M LinkedIn posts (2026): best window weekdays 3–8 p.m. local; top slots Wed 4 p.m., Fri 3–4 p.m.; mornings (6–11 a.m.) weaker; Monday weakest. Document/carousel posts get up to 596% more engagement than text-only. https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-linkedin/
- Sprout Social, ~2B engagements, 27 Nov 2025 to 27 Feb 2026. LinkedIn (all industries): Tue 11 a.m.–5 p.m., Wed 11 a.m.–4 p.m., Thu 11 a.m. and 1–5 p.m., Mon 1–2 p.m., Fri 11 a.m. and 1–2 p.m., weekends avoid. https://sproutsocial.com/best-times-to-post/linkedin-tech
- Sprout Social, Instagram 2026: Mon 2–4 p.m., Tue 1–7 p.m., Wed 12–9 p.m. (+11 p.m.), Thu 12–2 p.m., Fri no peak, weekends weakest; 3–7 a.m. is a dead zone. Reels follow the same windows; the algorithm weighs when your audience is active over format. https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-Instagram

## How we read them
- Both LinkedIn studies agree on **weekday early afternoon** (overlap ≈ 13:00–15:00 local). Neither supports 08:30–08:45. Old 08:45 slots are wrong.
- Studies are Western (Mon–Fri week). Israel works Sun–Thu, so for Israeli audiences we map Western Mon–Fri onto Israeli Sun–Thu. This mapping is our assumption, not a measured result: replace it with our own analytics as soon as we have 4+ weeks of data.
- US audiences: use the same local windows in US Eastern time (13:00–15:00 ET = 20:00–22:00 Israel).
- Nothing on Fri/Sat for Israeli audiences (weakest days, and Shabbat).

## Format for reach
- LinkedIn: a document/carousel (PDF) when the post teaches something; one post a day per profile; never two posts within 12 hours on the same profile.
- Instagram: Reels under 60 s; the hook in the first second; captions that invite sends/saves; the same time windows as above.
