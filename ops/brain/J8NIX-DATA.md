## Dependencies and the lead order (J8NIX 6.0, 14 Nov 2026) — keeps "Do this first" right without Dave

The app puts ONE item on top: the open thing only Dave can do that unlocks the most. Order: actionable first → **unlocks** (how many open items it blocks, counted through chains) → money (project at stage ≥ 4, or in Revenue/Finance) → deadline (🔴 now, a real due date, 🟠 today) → age. • Any item can wait on any other: `blocked_by text[]` on public.j8_tasks, public.j8_approvals and public.j8_projects holds ids (t-… task, p-… project, or an approval id). Only write a dependency that is stated: in the item itself (its result, warn, context or Dave's words), or in an open blocker in ops/brain/brain.json loose_ends (put that loose-end id in the task's source). ## Memory and chat understanding (17 Nov 2026) — what J8 remembers

2. Remembers: one j8_memory row per durable thing said (a decision, a fact about the business, a preference, a person, a goal, an idea), in the member's words, kind + 2–5 keyword tags, source_message_id.
