# 3S Brand OS · P0 Research (14 Nov 2026)

> Research department, run of 2026-11-14. Every fact below comes from a page that was opened during this run (see Sources). Lines marked **[Inference]** are our own reading of those facts, not sourced claims. This file contains no 3S prices: the price ranges here are market benchmarks only.

## Verdict (10 lines)

1. **Niche: keep "funded Israeli startups, Seed to Series B", keep HealthTech as the first wedge, add AI/cyber B2B as the second ring.** Israeli startups raised $7.6B in H1 2026 (+52%), but seed/early-stage got only $719M in Q2 and cyber took 34% of the money, so the early-stage pool is thinner than the headline. [Inference: HealthTech is where claim-checking QA matters most. AI/cyber B2B is where the money is.]
2. **Positioning line [Inference]:** "Your brand department, already trained on your brand: AI makes it, a scorer checks it against your rules, a senior creative director signs it off, and you can see how ChatGPT describes you."
3. **Gap 1, scored QA for startups.** Scored brand checking exists, but only in enterprise suites (Adobe GenStudio compliance %, brand.ai risk scores, Frontify Brand Check, Marq Brand Guardian, Markup.ai for text), all demo- or sales-led. Nothing found packages it for Seed to Series B.
4. **Gap 2, a done-for-you service with a measurable quality gate.** Design subscriptions run from about $449/mo to $15,000/mo plus fees (Superside), and none publishes an automatic brand score per asset. Tools such as Canva, Lovart and Claude Design make assets but leave QA and taste to the customer.
5. **Gap 3, AI visibility tied to the brand genome and to claims.** The visibility trackers (Profound, Peec, Otterly, Semrush) measure mentions but do not produce on-brand assets. GEOGrow comes closest ("Brand DNA" plus tracking) but has no human creative-director gate. For HealthTech, nobody combines visibility with sourced-claims QA.
6. **Name:** don't use "Brand OS" as the public product name, because brand.ai already sells a product called **"Brand OS"** in the same category. Recommended working name: **Brandplumb**. We found no product conflict, and on 14 Nov 2026 RDAP showed both brandplumb.com and brandplumb.ai unregistered. Keep "3S Brand OS" as the internal codename. **A real trademark search (ILPO + USPTO + EUIPO, classes 9/35/42) is still required.**
7. **Pricing guidance (market ranges, not 3S prices):**
   - Productized design subscriptions: about $449 to $4,995/mo, with Superside starting at $15,000/mo ([ManyPixels index](https://www.manypixels.co/blog/get-a-designer/design-subscription-pricing-index), [Superside](https://superside.com/pricing), [Designjoy](https://designjoy.co/)).
   - AI-visibility tools: $95 to $495/mo self-serve, enterprise custom ([Peec](https://www.peec.ai/pricing), [Semrush $99](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit), [Profound](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/profound-pricing)).
   - Startup identity projects: $5k to $20k at a studio, $25k to $50k at a startup branding agency ([Branx](https://thebranx.com/blog/startup-branding-cost-guide), [ManyPixels](https://www.manypixels.co/blog/brand-design/brand-identity-design-cost)).
   - [Inference] A sprint plus subscription fits between the design-subscription and visibility-tool bands.
8. **Biggest risk 1, platforms moving in.** Claude Design (brand-locked design systems with self-checking, June 2026), Figma agents and skills (March 2026) and Canva's brand intelligence are making on-brand generation a commodity. [Inference: the moat has to be the scorer, the human gate, visibility and claims, not generation.]
9. **Biggest risk 2, HealthTech claims liability.** FDA warning letters (SeniorLife, Whoop) were triggered by marketing and labeling claims. Israel's MoH can restrict medical-device advertising. Every health claim needs a source and a human legal OK.
10. **Biggest risk 3, outreach and law.** Under Israeli spam law (Communications Law §30A), each unlawful message can cost up to NIS 1,000 with no proof of harm. A single consent-request offer to a *business* is the only cold exception. LinkedIn stays manual. If EU audiences see our output, the EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency duties have applied since 2 Aug 2026.

## 3. The niche: evidence of pain

**Israeli funding volume (2026)**
- H1 2026: $7.6B raised by private Israeli tech (+52% YoY); Q2 $4.2B across only 97 rounds ([Globes, 2 Jul 2026](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001547660), IVC-LeumiTech).
- Seed and early-stage raised $719M in Q2, down from >$1B in each of the 3 prior quarters. Growth and late stage took ~83% of Q2 capital. Cyber took $2.57B (34%) (same source).
- Jan–Aug 2026 total ≈ $9.6B; 2025 full year $10.7B ([VINnews citing Globes/IVC](https://vinnews.com/2026/09/01/israeli-startups-raise-9-6-billion-in-first-eight-months-of-2026/)).
- Israeli mental-health tech: $352M raised in 2025 (+150%), across 178 companies, ~80% early stage ([Ynet/Media Line, Mental Health Innovation Map 2026](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/skqnxx9u11g)).
- [Inference] Fewer, bigger rounds mean the reachable list of freshly funded Seed–B companies is small, so it should be built deal by deal from news, not assumed to be large.

**Brand inconsistency at AI-content speed**
- 25% of marketers knowingly publish off-brand AI content under deadline pressure. 76% spend 3+ hours a week fixing AI output (Optimizely/Savanta, n=2,003, Jun 2026, [CMOtech](https://cmotech.uk/story/optimizely-study-finds-marketers-cut-corners-on-ai)).
- 62% rate brand-reputation damage from AI content as a moderate-to-high risk. 77% rely on manual spot checks. Only 45% trust AI to check its own content (Markup.ai report, n=135).
- Founders often ship UI "designed by your founder or CTO using an off the shelf UI kit", and design hires are hard to find early ([Seedcamp](https://seedcamp.com/views/hiring-for-design-part-1-hiring-your-first-designer/)). No survey data found on the share of Seed–B startups without a designer: **gap, not claimed.**

**AI search visibility**
- 51% of B2B software buyers now start research with an AI chatbot more often than Google (G2, n=1,076, Mar 2026).
- Marketscale (Jul 2026) reports that nearly 3/4 of B2B buyers consult ChatGPT during evaluation, citing Forrester, and that 51% of B2B tech brands have zero citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. The exact Forrester figure is unclear (72% vs 74%); treat as directional.
- Healthcare: in a 10-pharma AI-search study, nih.gov held 51% of citations and corporate domains only 1.3–1.8% each ([Worldcom, Jul 2026](https://www.worldcomgroup.com/insights/2026-healthcare-monitor-pharmaceutical-brands-ai-search/)).

**HealthTech marketing-claim constraints**
- US: FDA warning letters to SeniorLife (Aug 2025) cited claims such as "Detect early signs of Alzheimer's", "Proven to reduce falls by 58%" and implied FDA approval. Whoop (Jul 2025): a "wellness" feature was judged diagnostic. Lessons: no implied FDA endorsement, and diagnostic claims need premarket review ([HLC](https://hlc.com/en/publications/fda-enforcement-against-seniorlife-whoop-signal-new-line-in-the-sand-for-ai-health-apps)).
- Israel: the MoH regulates drug advertising (Rx ads to the public not allowed; OTC ads need prior approval) and may set conditions on medical-device advertising within registration (AMAR) ([Legal500 Israel Life Sciences](https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/israel-life-sciences/)).
- [Inference] This makes a "claims ledger" (every claim linked to a source, with human legal sign-off) the strongest HealthTech differentiator, and a liability if done badly.

## 4. What puts us 3–5 steps ahead [Inference, built on §1–3]

1. **A published, explainable Brand Consistency Score.** Adobe shows a % but doesn't publish the weighting. Marq has no numeric score. Frontify's "ratings" aren't explained. A transparent rubric (colour ΔE, font, logo clear-space, voice, unsourced claims) shown before and after on the client's own material is a visible difference.
2. **A scorer plus a named senior human gate as the product,** not as a cost. Tools leave QA to the customer, and agencies don't measure. Selling "nothing below threshold ever reaches you, and a 20-year creative director signs off" is unclaimed at startup price points.
3. **A claims ledger for regulated startups.** Every claim in every asset carries a source, and anything unsourced fails QA automatically. Adobe links to "disclosure libraries" for enterprise only; nothing similar found for startups.
4. **AI visibility in a closed loop with the genome.** Visibility tools measure, and the fix (site copy, structured data, earned sources) is produced by the same brand agents and passes the same QA. GEOGrow is the only partial overlap found.
5. **Build on the platforms, don't fight them.** Figma MCP and Skills, Claude Design design systems and Brandfetch data become delivery rails. The genome exports to them, which helps retention.

## 5. Legal and platform lines

- **Israeli anti-spam (Communications Law §30A):**
  - Ads by email, SMS, WhatsApp or fax need prior consent.
  - Each message must start with "פרסומת" (in the subject line for email) and include the advertiser's name and contact details, plus a same-channel opt-out and a web link for removal.
  - Damages up to NIS 1,000 per message without proof of harm.
  - **One-time exception:** a single offer *to a business* asking it to agree to receive ads is not a violation ([Tel Aviv Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/74023/115485/)).
  - [Inference] Outreach to named people at startups should be personal, non-promotional 1:1 notes; any mass or templated ad requires consent. Have a lawyer confirm whether an individual's work email counts as a "business" recipient.
- **Trademark check method:**
  1. ILPO free online search (identical, similar per class, by owner) at trademarks.justice.gov.il ([TAU](https://en-law.tau.ac.il/Trademarks/about_Trademark), [RCIP](https://www.rcip.co.il/en/service/trademarks/)).
  2. USPTO and EUIPO/TMview in Nice classes 9 (software), 35 (advertising/business services) and 42 (SaaS/design).
  3. Common-law web and app-store search.
  4. Counsel opinion before filing.
  
  ® may only be used on registered marks in Israel (RCIP).
- **AI-content disclosure:** EU AI Act Art. 50 applies from 2 Aug 2026 and was not delayed by the Digital Omnibus. Providers must machine-mark AI-generated content (legacy systems have until 2 Dec 2026). Deployers must disclose deepfakes and AI text on public-interest matters unless it was human-reviewed under editorial responsibility. Fines up to €15M or 3% of turnover ([Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html?id=102nbon)). Adobe states that customers stay responsible for their own legal compliance.
  - [Inference] Contracts should say the client is the publisher. Our human gate gives the "editorial control" posture. Keep C2PA or other marking from upstream generators intact.
