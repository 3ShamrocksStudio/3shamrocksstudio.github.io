## Verdict (10 lines)

1. **None of the three seeds qualifies as given.** Ranked: **N1 Elder-care benefits co-pilot (new) > Seed 1 rights/money recovery (only as a self-filing variant) > Seed 3 SH✡MER AI emergency layer (B2G, human sale) > Seed 2 company factory (kill).**
2. **Conditional winner, N1:** an AI that runs the whole long-term-care benefits journey for an ageing parent's family (NII nursing benefit, upgrade requests, health-fund group long-term-care insurance), filed by the family itself, priced on outcome plus a monitoring subscription. It is a sharp vertical of seed 1, not a new idea category.
3. **Why N1:** ~392,000 eligible seniors (end 2025); 134,314 new claims and 158,905 upgrade requests in one year; only 57.8% of new claims and 43.0% of upgrades approved; health-fund policies pay ₪3,200–₪10,000/month for 5 years; Israel assesses 97% of cases on documents alone. Families are drowning in forms and the money per family is large.
4. **Seed 1 as pitched is already a business, not a disruption:** refund firms take 15–20% of the refund; Taxtica already sells "AI-native" refunds with a CPA filing; the Tax Authority sends its own "green envelopes", blocked bot-driven firms and requires the client's active consent before a representative can file. Only a "file it yourself, AI does the work" variant is new, and its moat is thin (the state could make refunds automatic).
5. **Seed 2 is already done and funded:** Polsia ($30M at $250M, ~$10M ARR claimed and publicly disputed) and NanoCorp (11,000+ companies, ~$800K ARR, about $73 per company per year). Anthropic's own AI-run shop still needed humans. Dave has $0 revenue to prove a factory works. Kill.
6. **Seed 3 has a real need, but the sale is not autonomous:** Carbyne (AI agent at 300 emergency centres, Tel Aviv and Jerusalem), Aurelian (74% of non-emergency calls automated) prove AI triage; councils buy through people. Best wedge is **N2**: the 64 of 255 Israeli authorities with no phone hotline (Arab authorities 36% coverage). Keep it as a separate human-sold track (autonomy 4).
7. **Dave's edge is strongest in N1:** VIBRANT companion app (2× retention, TIME Best Inventions 2023) is exactly "keep a stressed, older-user family engaged through a long medical process"; seed 2 and the tax variant use none of it.
8. **Legal gate for N1 (before any build):** NII law caps paid "assistance or representation" fees (10.25–12.25% for disability claims; lawyers only at medical committees). Need one legal check on whether nursing claims fall under it and whether a family-filed, AI-prepared claim counts as "assistance". Medical data is sensitive under Amendment 13. Hard rule: accurate documentation only, never inflating. The Comptroller found NII rated higher than the health fund in 75% of paired cases, and NII is moving to in-person and AI checks.
9. **Zero-budget path [Inference]:** Claude builds the chat intake, document reader, claim-pack generator and upgrade-trigger monitor in 2–3 weeks. Free "is mum getting the right level?" checker plus caregiver content brings families in (no cold sends). First paid monitoring subscriber around day 30–45; first success fee about 2–4 months after filing.
10. **Kill rule:** by day 60, ≥ 30 families finished intake unaided and ≥ 5 paying, or stop. Dave's only steps: a go/no-go and OK on public price and terms. The legal check needs his OK if it costs anything.

## Seed 1 · AI rights/money recovery in Israel (מיצוי זכויות), success-fee only

**The money is real (sourced).**
- Tax: the State Comptroller found **hundreds of thousands of families overpay about ₪1bn a year**; as of Feb 2021 the Tax Authority estimated it owed **~₪3.6bn for 2013–2019 across ~2 million cases**; **435,546 families** had not filed for 2016 (≈ ₪803M) (Comptroller 2021). The Tax Authority's own outreach found **405,000** unfiled 2016–2018 cases; only **34,000** claimed **₪123M** (≈ ₪3,600 each); ~305,000 workers (~₪1bn) still unclaimed for 2017–2018 (Maariv, Jan 2024). Tax Authority data: **8 in 10 employees are entitled to a refund**; 6 years back; 4%/yr interest (Bizportal 2026).
- NII benefits: take-up is near 100% for automatic, age-based benefits but **"considerably less than half"** of potential for unemployment and income support (NII journal, Gottlieb). Reservist pay: NII paid 90% of eligible self-employed/student reservists automatically in 2023 (NII, Nov 2023). **[Inference: reservist money is mostly already automated; low upside.]**
- Pension/insurance: Har HaKesef and Har HaBituach are **free government tools** (since 2013); the clearinghouse spans >₪2 trillion; the regulator **fined an insurance agency ₪1M** over paid intermediaries in this space (Makor Rishon 2022).

**Existing players.** Many refund firms at **15–20% of the refund**, some with upfront fees (taxes-refund.co.il); **Taxtica** pitches "first AI-native tax refund platform", AI reads documents, CPA files, "no refund, no pay" (F4 Fund page); Tax Authority online Form 135 since 2019, a simulator, "green envelopes" to hundreds of thousands (Maariv); free Kol Zchut. US analogue **Turnout** ($21M seed, AI orchestrator automates ~60% of tasks, licensed professionals sign off, >$40M returned) (Turnout release).

**Legal limits.** Representing a taxpayer before the Tax Authority is reserved for licensed representatives (tax advisor; CPA without restriction) (Protocol, citing the tax-advisor representation law). Since Dec 2022 a refund-only POA takes effect **only after the client actively approves it** in their personal area (Tax Authority letter). The Tax Authority cut off representatives over **"prohibited bots"** and one AI-era firm closed new sign-ups (Globes 2023). NII caps paid assistance/representation fees by law (10.25–12.25% for disability claims; flat ₪907 fees for others; lawyers-only stages) (NII fee table, 2024). Amendment 13 privacy duties apply (see OPPORTUNITY-SCAN). 

**How much is truly automatable [Inference].** Discovery (read 106 forms, payslips, life events), calculation and Form 135 drafting: ~90%. Filing *as a representative*: needs a licensed CPA, active client consent, no bots → autonomy 4. Filing *by the user* with the AI preparing everything: autonomy 8, no licence needed for the user's own filing (needs legal confirmation that preparing a self-filed form is not regulated "tax advice").

- **Makes obsolete:** the 15–20% fee for simple salaried refunds.
- **Why incumbents don't own it:** their margin is the percentage; Taxtica is the closest and could cut price any time.
- **Why it's weak as a moonshot:** crowded, single-shot (not recurring), the state is the long-run competitor (Comptroller already told it to approach taxpayers proactively), and Dave has no tax edge.
- **Use:** fold the self-check into N1 as an "also check" module for the same families.

## Seed 2 · "Company factory" (autonomous agents turn a niche into a running micro-business)

- **Already done:** **Polsia**, launched late 2025, $49/month + 20% of revenue + 20% of ad spend, ~7,600 customers, raised **$30M at $250M**, ARR "approaching $10M" by founder claim; critics estimate ~$4.6M subscription ARR (Fanatical Futurist, May 2026). A pseudonymous investigation alleges manual operation and misleading ARR (unverified) (Feedbagel). **NanoCorp:** free tier, **11,000+ companies, ~$800K ARR**; production-ready only for content, outreach, tier-1 support (Noqta guide, May 2026). **[Inference: ~$73/company/year means almost all "companies" earn nothing.]** Builders: Replit ~$525M annualised (Sacra, Apr 2026); Lovable in the same class.
- **What is genuinely unsolved:** demand. AI can ship a site, Stripe tiers and outreach; it cannot make a niche want the product. Anthropic's Project Vend phase 2: the AI shop became profitable only with procedures, still needed humans for stocking, delivery and difficult customers, nearly signed an illegal contract, and its "CEO" agent approved lenient requests ~8× more than it refused (Anthropic).
- **Autonomy 9 on paper; real outcome unproven.** Legal: claims of autonomy are a consumer-protection risk (FTC vs DoNotPay, $193K, Jan 2025).
- **Dave's edge:** none that the funded players lack; our own funnel (72 sends → 0 calls) is the opposite of proof.
- **Verdict: kill as a product.** Keep the internal agent stack for running 3S and N1.

## Seed 3 · SH✡MER as an AI civilian-emergency layer for councils/communities (subscription)

- **What AI makes possible:** 24/7 multilingual intake, triage and routing without a staffed room. Proof: Carbyne's AI agent answers "when no human calltaker is available" (≈300 sites, 7,000 calltakers, 23 US states; $100M raised Jul 2025; works with Tel Aviv and Jerusalem) (Globes). Aurelian automates **~74% of non-emergency calls** (Pulse 2.0) and runs live in a dozen+ US centres; Hyper and Prepared compete (TechCrunch).
- **Israel gaps:** **64 of 255 authorities have no phone hotline**; Arab authorities only **36%** (IDI, Jan 2024). Security coordinators relied on council-bought radios; military phones went only to council-level coordinators; the Comptroller recommends coordination, not an app (Comptroller 2026, security coordinators). Free/hackathon tools appear in each war (Simplex 3D free to authorities in 2023; Guardia squad-location prototype 2024). Salesforce Agentforce is approved for government use (Calcalist, Jun 2026).
- **Who pays:** councils, under the tender-exemption cap (see OPPORTUNITY-SCAN B). **Procurement is human:** meetings, champions, contracts → autonomy **4** (delivery and support can be AI; sale and invoicing cannot).
- **Why incumbents don't own small authorities [Inference]:** Carbyne sells to emergency centres and big cities; US AI-911 firms aren't in Hebrew/Arabic or Israeli procurement; MyCity is a reporting app, not a triage agent.
- **N2 wedge [Inference]:** "Moked-in-a-box": an AI WhatsApp/voice hotline in Arabic and Hebrew for authorities with none, that switches to emergency mode (shelters, evacuees, volunteer squads) and feeds the SH✡MER Command dashboard. It replaces a staffed 106 at a fraction of the cost. Risk: Arab authorities' budgets; long cycles.
- **Verdict:** strongest asset, real need, **fails the full-autonomy filter.** Run it as Dave's human-sold B2G track, not the moonshot.

## N1 (new) · Elder-care benefits co-pilot (the conditional winner)

**What it is [Inference].** A family member opens a Hebrew (later Russian/Arabic) chat about an ageing parent. The agent interviews them, reads uploaded medical letters and health-fund assessments, checks the income test, builds the NII nursing-benefit claim or upgrade request (בקשת החמרה) with the right evidence, prepares the family for an in-person assessment, and opens the health-fund group long-term-care insurance claim in parallel. It then **monitors**: reminders, a new upgrade request when the condition changes, private-policy deadlines, and a tax-refund "also check". The family submits on the official sites; the AI does the work.

**Sourced facts.**
- Scale: eligible seniors **~180,000 (2018) → ~392,000 (2025)**, ~30% of retirement-age citizens (Comptroller 2026, ageing). **375,134** recipients Jan 2025; **134,314 new claims** and **158,905 upgrade requests** Nov 2023–Oct 2024; approval **57.8%** (new) and **43.0%** (upgrade) (NII research, Feb 2025).
- Money: cash benefit **₪1,708 (level 1) to ₪7,452 (level 6)** per month; health-fund group LTC policies pay **₪5,000/₪4,100/₪3,200** a month at home or **₪10,000/₪6,500/₪4,500** in an institution, for **5 years** after 60 days; group LTC claims approved rose **8,195 → 20,711 (2012–2023)**; health-fund criteria tightened in early 2025 to 4 of 6 daily activities (Bizportal Sep 2026; Comptroller 2026).
- Process pain: **97% of NII assessments are document-based**; document-assessed claims were approved ~16 points more often; the Comptroller found NII rated need higher than a health fund in **75% of 10,523 paired cases**; NII is reviewing in-person assessments and considering video and AI tools (Bizportal Sep 2026; Comptroller 2026). NII spending ~₪7bn → ₪21.1bn.
- Fees: NII law caps paid "assistance or representation" fees, e.g. disability claims **10.25–12.25%** of 60 months' benefit; some stages lawyers-only (NII fee table, 2024). Nursing is not in the table we opened. **Must be confirmed.**

**Scoring.**
- **Makes obsolete:** paying a human rep a percentage to fill nursing and LTC forms; the "who do we even call" weeks. **[Inference: 10× cheaper per family, same-day packet.]**
- **Who pays:** adult children (35–65) of ageing parents. Outcome fee below the legal cap + monthly monitoring subscription (recurring, fits Dave's preference). Price set privately, not in this file.
- **Competitors:** human rights-recovery firms and lawyers (% fee), care companies that help clients file (they gain from approved hours [Inference]), Kol Zchut (free info, no filing). No AI-native end-to-end product found in this run.
- **Why incumbents can't copy fast [Inference]:** firms sell human hours at capped percentages; care companies have a conflict of interest; government tools inform but don't prepare evidence packs; a general AI chatbot doesn't know NII's evidence logic, health-fund policy rules and the monitoring triggers.
- **Autonomy 7:** sales inbound (free checker, caregiver content, "share with your sibling" loop); delivery and support by AI; billing by card. Humans: the family (end user) and a partner lawyer only for medical committees and courts (lawyer-only by law).
- **Legal blockers:** fee-law scope for nursing and what counts as "assistance"; Amendment 13 (medical data = sensitive; minimal storage, consent, security); never advise inflating. **Policy risk:** NII is tightening document-based assessment, so the product must be "accurate and complete evidence + assessment prep", which stays valuable when assessments move in person.
- **Time/cost to first paying user [Inference]:** 2–3 weeks build, ₪0 beyond existing tools; first subscriber day 30–45; first outcome fee 2–4 months.
- **Dave's unfair advantage:** a decade of designing patient companions that keep people engaged through long medical journeys (VIBRANT 2×, TIME 2023), HealthTech credibility for trust-sensitive families, Hebrew-native copy, and an agent stack that can run it.
- **12-month view [Inference]:** ~293,000 NII filings a year; capturing 0.5% ≈ 1,450 families/yr; with a monitoring subscription most revenue becomes recurring. Expansion: Russian and Arabic, disability (נכות כללית), then the families' own tax refunds.

## N3 (checked, rejected) · consumer/spam small-claims engine

Statutory spam damages are up to ₪1,000 per message, but a court awarded ₪7,800 on 39 messages (~₪200 each) against a ₪33,000 claim (Mako). In the US the FTC made DoNotPay pay $193K and banned unsupported "robot lawyer" claims (Jan 2025). Small upside, practice-of-law exposure, no Dave edge.
