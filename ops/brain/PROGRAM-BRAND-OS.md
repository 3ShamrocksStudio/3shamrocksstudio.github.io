## 1. What it is (one sentence)
**An autonomous brand department for startups: AI agents produce everything a brand needs, every piece is checked against the client's own brand rules before anyone sees it, and the founder commands it all from one place.**

## 2. Why it wins (what clients can see and understand)
- **Brand Consistency Score.** Every asset gets a score against the client's brand rules (logo use, colour, type, layout, voice, claims). Clients see the before and after on their own material.
- **AI Visibility Score.** How the brand shows up when people ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity about their category. Measured monthly, with fixes.
- **Speed with no drop in quality.** Requests turn into on-brand assets in hours, not weeks, and nothing below the quality bar ever ships.
- **Human command.** The client (and Dave as creative director) approves, edits or kills anything from one screen. The AI never publishes on its own.

## 3. The five modules
1. **Brand Genome:** the 3SDS method turns a brand into machine-readable rules: tokens, logo rules, type, layout grids, voice, words to use and avoid, approved claims with sources.
2. **Production agents:** social posts, ads, decks, landing sections, emails and product screens, all generated from the genome.
3. **Precision QA gate:** an automatic scorer checks every asset against the genome (colour distance, font, logo clear-space, voice, unsourced claims). Below the threshold, it goes back automatically. Above it, Dave's taste gate gives the final OK.
4. **Command (J8NIX for clients):** chat with the brand agent, a "what's next" priority view, approve/kill, stats (consistency score, output, turnaround).
5. **AI Visibility:** monthly checks of the brand in AI answers, plus concrete fixes (site copy, structured data, sources).
