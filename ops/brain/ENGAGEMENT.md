# Engagement playbook per channel (Dave, 15 Nov 2026: "always research and find the best ways to maximize engagement and apply it every time, for every channel")

Binds every post and outreach draft. Update when a source shows a change; date each change.

## LinkedIn posts (checked 15 Nov 2026)
- Hook in the first 1-2 lines (before "see more", ~200 characters): a sharp question or a surprising claim the reader feels about themselves ("האתר שלכם באמת נגיש כמו שאתם חושבים?").
- No external link in the post body: posts with links are shown to fewer people. Put the link in the first comment, posted right after publishing, and say "link in the first comment".
- Always a native visual: a 4:5 image (1080x1350) or a PDF carousel; carousels/documents get the most engagement in 2026 reports. Studio look: black #0a0910, violet #8239ea, Heebo.
- End with a question that invites comments; reply to every comment in the first hour.
- 3-5 hashtags at the end. Short lines, white space, simple words.
- Time: see POSTING-TIMES.md. Post by hand, never automated.
Sources: https://www.agilitypr.com/pr-news/social-media-influencer-marketing/the-linkedin-link-tax-why-sharing-your-blog-is-costing-you-60-percent-of-your-audience/ ; https://dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now ; https://nealschaffer.com/linkedin-post/

## LinkedIn connect notes
- Warm and short (under 200 characters, free-account limit): greet, congratulate or show interest, one line on Dave, ask to connect. Never explain their product back to them (Dave, 15 Nov).

## Email first contact
- Open with them (thanks / what they do well), name their real pain in their own world, then the offer as the answer, one small ask (10-minute call). Israeli law lines (פרסומת, address, remove line) always stay.
