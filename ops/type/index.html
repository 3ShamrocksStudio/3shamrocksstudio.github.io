Three faces only we have.

Each direction is a display typeface of our own, cut from open-licence letters and reshaped so no other site uses it, with a brighter colour world around it. Each one is shown on the studio home and on the SH✡MER Nature page, in English and Hebrew.

1 · LookScroll A, B and C on your phone.
2 · PickReply in J8NIX: “A”, “B” or “C”, plus anything to change.
3 · ThenThe team finishes the full typeface and rolls it out everywhere.

3S Cut

Confident craft. Every letter has two diagonal cuts, like a designer’s blade, and the dots on i, j and ! are tiny shamrocks.
