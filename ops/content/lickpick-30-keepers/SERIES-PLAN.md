# 30 Keepers: LickPick's daily reel series

**What it is:** 30 short reels. Each one teaches a single lick worth keeping.
**Why this name:** "Keep" is LickPick's own word. The app stamps every jam Keep, Review or Trash, and the landing line is "Keep the licks." Each episode ends with the lick getting a KEEP stamp, so the series name, the payoff and the product all say the same thing.

- **Header on screen:** `KEEPER 07/30 · 30 licks worth keeping`
- **Account:** @lickpick.app. This is the handle in the drafts at `ops/ig-drafts/2026-11-12/index.html` (LickPick section, "Awaiting Dave's OK"). **NEEDS YOUR EYES:** please confirm the account exists, is logged in, and is a Business or Creator account. "Comment LICK" can only send DMs automatically if it is.
- **Status:** LickPick is in staging and closed beta. No reel says "launched" or "live".

## Format (the same every day, so viewers learn it)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–1 s | **Hook.** A one-word topic slams in (HOME, BLUE, FLIP…), a guitar stab plays, and a hook line says what you'll get. |
| 1–8 s | **Lesson.** The fretboard draws in and the notes pop in on the beat as you hear them. Roots show as LickPick picks. Note chips and one technical caption line follow. |
| 8–14 s | **The lick.** Numbered dots light up as each note plays and a path draws through them. The lick always ends on a target note. |
| ~13 s | **Payoff.** A KEEP stamp lands, plus a one-line "why it works". |
| 15–21 s | **Tie-in and CTA.** An amp-strip wipe leads to the LickPick end card: "Record yourself playing it. LickPick picks your best lick." A lick card replays the lick with a waveform, then the stage CTA and tomorrow's teaser. |

**Rules for every episode:**
- Every fret and note is checked by `theory.py` (string+fret → note in EADGBE, with assertions). An episode can't render if a note is wrong.
- The audio uses the same note data as the picture.
- The audio is original: GeneralUser GS v2.0.3, −14 LUFS.
- Episodes 1–3 are in A, so viewers can stack them. Later episodes change key on purpose.
