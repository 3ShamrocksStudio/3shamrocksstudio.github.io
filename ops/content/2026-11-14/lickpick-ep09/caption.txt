The 3-note lick every rock solo uses. Three notes in a triplet, looped until it burns.

Play B string fret 8 (G), high e fret 5 (A), B string fret 5 (E). That is three notes per beat, then straight back to the start: G, A, E, G, A, E. The lick loops it four times, then lands on A at high e fret 5, the same A the loop passes through. All of it sits in box 1 of A minor pentatonic, frets 5 to 8.
