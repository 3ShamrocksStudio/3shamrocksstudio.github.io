KEEPER 08/30 · SLIDE

Stuck in one box? One slide on the G string moves you from box 1 into box 2 in the middle of a lick.

Slide the G string from fret 7 (D) up to fret 9 (E). Fret 7 is in both boxes; fret 9 is only in box 2. The lick: D string fret 7 (A), G string fret 5, G string fret 7 sliding up to 9, B string fret 8, high e frets 8 and 10, then it lands on A at B string fret 10, an octave above where it started.

Save this for the next time your solo feels boxed in.
