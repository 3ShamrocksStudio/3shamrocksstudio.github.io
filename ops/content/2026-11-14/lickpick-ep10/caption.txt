KEEPER 10/30 · PAIR

Two strings. One Chuck Berry. Two notes struck together hit harder than one.

Use only the G and B strings. Pair 1: G string fret 5 and B string fret 5 (C + E). Pair 2: G string fret 7 and B string fret 8 (D + G). Strike each pair together and alternate them: C+E, D+G, C+E, D+G. Then land on A at high e fret 5. All of it sits in box 1 of A minor pentatonic, frets 5 to 8, over an A7 shuffle.

Save this for the next time your single notes feel thin.
