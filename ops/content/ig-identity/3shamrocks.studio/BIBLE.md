# @3shamrocks.studio · Instagram identity bible (v1, 17 Nov 2026)
Sources: `sprint/index.html` tokens · `assets/studio/3shamrocks.png` (PNG logo) · `work/*.html` case facts · BRAIN §−6.L.

**Audience.** Founders and product leads (Israel + abroad) about to launch or fix a product. They scroll for proof of craft, not for "what a studio is". They judge in 2 seconds whether the work looks senior.
**Positioning.** A one-founder design studio (since 2007) that takes a rough idea to a product people love: brand, app and system, end to end.
**Voice** (English on slides, Hebrew first line in captions; always first person "I").
- Do: show the decision and why ("Red has one job"). · Do: name real roles and real results (TIME Best Inventions 2023, VIBRANT). · Do: short, calm, exact sentences.
- Don't: "we", hype, "game-changer". · Don't: stats or claims without a source file. · Don't: explain the studio instead of showing work.

**Colour roles.** Ground `#000` · surface `#0d0a17` · ink `#f6f4ff` / ink-2 76% / ink-3 56% · accent violet-2 `#b07cff` (one word or line per frame) · violet `#8239ea` (rules, buttons) · shamrock green `#8be36a` only inside the logo. Hairlines `rgba(255,255,255,.12)`. Product colours appear only inside the artefact being shown.
**Type roles.** Display: Bricolage Grotesque 700, −0.035em, 96–104 px. Text: Instrument Sans 400–600, 28–34 px. Labels: Instrument Sans 600, 22 px, +0.18em caps. Radius 3 px everywhere.
**Grid look.** Black gallery wall: each tile = one artefact on black with a hairline frame, big type, lots of air. Rows alternate artefact / decision / proof.
**Imagery.** Real artefacts only: logos, badges, brand books, real screens shown flat with a hairline border (never a phone mockup), process sketches, before/after. Background: faint 108 px hairline grid. Mini-me avatar (`assets/studio/minime_nobg.png`) only for studio-life posts, neutral expression, max 1 in 9 posts; never as filler, never in case studies. No real face of Dave without his OK.
**Icons.** Hairline outline 1.5 px, square caps, mitred corners, violet-2 on black (`icons/`).
**Assets.** Labels: "CASE STUDY 01" top-left + "03 / 08" top-right under a hairline. Callouts: 1 px violet line + caps label. Footer: PNG logo bottom-left, "Swipe →" bottom-right. No stickers, no badges.
**3 signature formats** (`samples/`).
1. *Case cover* 4:5: label row, one hero artefact centred (≤ 55% of height), 2-line headline, 1-line scope, footer.
2. *Design decision* 4:5: "Decision 0X · topic", headline that states the rule, the artefact proving it, one 2-line why.
3. *Proof reel cover* 9:16: role + verifiable result as the headline, artefact in a hairline frame, all text inside y 250–1500.
**Motion.** Hairlines draw, words rise 40 px with `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`, 0.7–0.9 s, 0.2 s stagger. Cuts on the beat; no spins, bounces or glitch.
**Sound.** Music: minimal ambient electronic, 84–96 BPM, felt piano, warm pad, soft sub, no drums or brushed only; calm and confident. Sting: piano C5–G5–E6 rising arpeggio + crystal shimmer (1.2 s), `sound/sting-12s.wav`. SFX: soft paper swipe (transition), muted wood tick (UI), single piano note + shimmer (success). Licence: original compositions; samples GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 (free for commercial music); stock music only from Pixabay Music (Pixabay licence) or YouTube Audio Library "no attribution".
**Speech.** Usually none (on-screen type carries it). If used: male, 40s, warm Israeli-accented English, slow; e.g. "This is how I made a safety app feel calm."
**CTA.** One quiet line: "Full case · 3shamrocks.com/work/…" or "Building something? Send me a DM." Never prices.
**Outreach voice** (DM, email, comments): warm, first person, short, asks one question. 1) Comment "Love the badge!" → "Thank you! The ✡ replacing the O was the first decision, everything grew from there." 2) DM "How much for a logo?" → "Happy to help. What are you building, and where are you stuck? I'd like to see it before I suggest anything."
**Banned.** Website screenshots in phone mockups · leftover UI ("Tap anywhere for sound", status bars) · purple-on-purple gradient backgrounds · the avatar with odd expressions or as filler · other accounts' looks · "we".
