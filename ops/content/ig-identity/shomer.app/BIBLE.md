# @shomer.app · Instagram identity bible (v1, 17 Nov 2026)
Sources: `/home/claude/shomer-app/shomer.html` `:root` (navy, gold, platinum, red) · `assets/work/shomer/` badge + logo · `work/shomer.html` facts · CLAUDE.md data and privacy policy.

**Audience.** Israeli families, parents of teens, older people living alone and the neighbours who care for them; Hebrew first (Arabic and English later). They want to feel safe, not scared.
**Positioning.** שומרים אחד על השני: one tap tells the people you chose, and SH✡MER users nearby, where you are.
**Voice** (Hebrew, warm, simple; RTL).
- Do: tell a human moment (the neighbour, the friend walking home). · Do: give one useful safety fact. · Do: always "קודם 100 · 101 · 102".
- Don't: fear, crime-scene drama or panic. · Don't: claim it replaces emergency services. · Don't: availability beyond the truth (Android app + web app in the browser; iPhone store app not out).

**Colour roles.** Navy scale `#060F1E`–`#2E4070` (ground, cards) · gold `#E0A828` / bright `#F5C840` (key word, hairline frames, bells) · platinum `#B6C2D6` (secondary lines, disclaimers) · text `#F0F4FF` / `#C8D4E8` · **red `#CC1426` only for SOS**, never decoration.
**Type roles.** Display: Frank Ruhl Libre 800, 76–96 px, Hebrew. Text/UI: Heebo 400–700, 30–44 px. Wordmark: always SH✡MER (the ✡ replaces the O).
**Grid look.** Warm night photography of real-life people (navy shadows, gold street light), alternating with engraved navy "know-how" cards.
**Imagery.** Hyper-realistic people scenarios (neighbours, friends, grandparents), warm and hopeful; AI-made images carry the on-image chip "תמונה שנוצרה ב-AI" and the IG "AI info" label. No real users, no faces of minors, no real addresses or maps of real people.
**Icons.** Gold 1.8 px round outline, platinum detail line, red only on SOS (`icons/`).
**Assets.** Double engraved frame (gold 1.5 px + platinum inner), badge as seal at the top of cards, logo PNG bottom, AI chip top corner.
**3 signature formats** (`samples/`).
1. *Real-life scene* 4:5: photo, navy fade on the lower 45%, 2-line Hebrew headline, 1 line of warmth, logo.
2. *Know-how card* 4:5: badge seal, engraved frame, one fact (e.g. 100/101/102), disclaimer line.
3. *Scene reel cover* 9:16: photo, Hebrew headline in y 1100–1450, AI chip.
**Motion.** Gold map-ping rings, slow push-ins on photos, soft blur-in of the badge, 0.8–1.2 s fades. Never shaky or flashing.
**Sound.** Music: warm cinematic, 68–82 BPM, strings, piano, choir pad, soft frame drum; hopeful Israeli warmth (oud or nylon guitar welcome). Sting: low strings swell + three gold bells F#–A–D (1.8 s), `sound/sting-12s.wav`. SFX: soft whoosh (transition), gentle bell tick (UI), warm chord (success); the SOS alarm sound only when showing SOS. Licence: original; samples GeneralUser GS v2.0.3.
**Speech.** Optional Hebrew VO: female or male 35–55, warm, native Israeli Hebrew, unhurried. "השכנה מקומה שלוש לא לבד."
**CTA.** "קישור בביו" · "הורידו לאנדרואיד או פתחו בדפדפן". No pressure lines.
**Outreach voice.** Hebrew, warm, respectful, plain. 1) Comment "זה מחליף את 100?" → "ממש לא. בחירום מחייגים קודם 100, 101 או 102. SH✡MER מוסיף את האנשים הקרובים אליכם." 2) DM "יש לאייפון?" → "באייפון אפשר כבר עכשיו באפליקציית הווב בדפדפן. אפליקציה בחנות בדרך."
**Banned.** Fear imagery, weapons, blood · red outside SOS · unlabelled AI people · "SHOMER" without the ✡ in text · any user data, names or precise locations.
