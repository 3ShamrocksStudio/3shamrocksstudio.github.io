# @trashure_hunters · Instagram identity bible (v1, 17 Nov 2026)
Sources: `th-site/index.html` `:root` (element colours, cream, ink, radius 28) + Fredoka/Inter · `th-site/assets/img/` (Elemonsters, King Grime, logo) · locked slogan and story facts.

**Audience.** Kids 8–14 and the parents and teachers who follow for them. Kids want heroes, monsters and a mission; parents want safe, green, screen-time-they-approve.
**Positioning.** An AR eco-adventure where kids catch litter, power up Elemonsters and beat King Grime. *Clean the World. Win the Game.*
**Voice** (English, kid-credible hero voice; parents' voice on safety posts).
- Do: short, active, celebratory ("Spot it. Snap it. Bin it."). · Do: give each Elemonster a personality. · Do: add "Ask a grown-up first" to any real-world mission.
- Don't: scare kids or shame them about pollution. · Don't: promise downloads or prizes (the app is not released). · Don't: collect or show kids' data or faces.

**Colour roles.** Cream `#FFF7E8` ground · ink `#14272E` outlines/text · night `#0A0A2E` villain posts · grime `#39038B` · element colours, one hero colour per post: space `#FC71EA`, air `#7F4DE2`, water `#3DA7F9`, earth `#28E4B1`, fire `#FF8400`, sun `#FFC83D`.
**Type roles.** Display: Fredoka 700, 100–150 px, with ink stroke + 8 px ink drop. Text: Inter 600, 30–34 px.
**Grid look.** Bright toy-box: cream tiles with one big Elemonster each, every third tile a night "King Grime" or mission card.
**Imagery.** The 3D Elemonsters, eggs, King Grime and litter props from the game art; blob shapes behind characters; hands-holding-phone AR shots without faces. Background textures: soft blobs, confetti dots.
**Icons.** Chunky filled shapes in element colours, 1.6 px ink outline, round corners, white gloss dot (`icons/`).
**Assets.** Rounded pill chips ("Meet the Elemonsters · 2 of 5"), game cards (28 px radius, 10 px ink drop), stars, XP chips without numbers.
**3 signature formats** (`samples/`).
1. *Meet the Elemonster* 4:5: blob + character, name in Fredoka, one fact.
2. *Mission card* 4:5: night card, 3-beat mission, litter props, safety line.
3. *Villain reel cover* 9:16: King Grime, cliffhanger line, slogan, logo.
**Motion.** Pop-in with overshoot (scale 0→1.12→1, 0.5 s), bouncy staggers, squash and stretch, sparkles on collect. Fast but readable.
**Sound.** Music: playful orchestral/marimba adventure, 120–132 BPM, marimba, pizzicato, glockenspiel, light kit. Sting: marimba run C–D–E–G–C + "collect" chime (1.6 s), `sound/sting-12s.wav`. SFX: whoosh-pop (transition), bubble tick (UI), coin-chime (success), King Grime = low tuba "bwomp". Licence: original; samples GeneralUser GS v2.0.3.
**Speech.** Optional VO: kid-friendly narrator, female or male 25–35, bright neutral English, medium pace. "Bloop lives in the water zone. Help it grow!"
**CTA.** "Follow the hunt" · "Which Elemonster are you? Comment!" No download CTA until release.
**Outreach voice.** Friendly to parents and teachers, clear on safety. 1) Comment "When can my son play?" → "Soon! The game is in its final build. Follow along and you'll see the launch first." 2) DM from a teacher → "Thank you for reaching out! I'd love to hear about your class. What age are they?"
**Banned.** Real kids' faces or names · scary gore or dirty-shaming · release dates or prizes not set · the 3Shamrocks studio look.
