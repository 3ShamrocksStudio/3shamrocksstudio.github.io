# @meetinq.ai (Inq) · Instagram identity bible (v1, 17 Nov 2026)
Sources: `n/meetinq/index.html` live palette (`#08090D`, `#19C3C9`, `#F1F4F5`, `#AEB8BC`, `#0D2226`) and `n/meetinq/logo.png` (glass octopus) · niche and voice from `/mnt/user-data/outputs/ds/meetinq.md`. Change: the old shared network type (Space Grotesk/Inter) made all 4 media accounts look alike, so Inq now has its own: Instrument Serif + Manrope. Its 2 Nov violet palette is replaced by the live teal of the logo and link page. No 3Shamrocks link anywhere.

**Audience.** Small-business owners (cafés, salons, clinics, makers) who DIY their brand with AI tools and want to look premium.
**Positioning.** Inq, the octopus art director: makes a small brand look expensive, with restraint and AI tools used honestly.
**Voice** (English; quiet, exacting, tasteful).
- Do: one before/after or one rule per post. · Do: explain why in one line. · Do: disclose tools and affiliate links (#ad).
- Don't: shout, emoji on images, exclamation marks. · Don't: invented client results. · Don't: designer jargon.

**Colour roles.** Ground `#08090D` · deep teal `#0D2226` (the "after" surface) · text `#F1F4F5` · grey `#AEB8BC` (labels) · **teal `#19C3C9`** one ink moment per frame (italic word, number, dot).
**Type roles.** Display: Instrument Serif 62–112 px, italic for the teal word. Text: Manrope 500, 28–32 px. Labels: Manrope 600, +0.32em caps.
**Grid look.** Dark gallery of type: before/after pairs, serif lists, one octopus tile per row.
**Imagery.** Real type and layout demos, product mock-ups of small brands (labelled "concept"), the glass octopus with teal light trails. Texture: none, just space.
**Icons.** Fine 1 px teal line art with one filled "ink drop" dot (`icons/`).
**Assets.** "BEFORE / AFTER" caps labels, teal hairline dividers, thin numbered lists, tentacle line flourish.
**3 signature formats** (`samples/`).
1. *Before / after* 4:5: two stacked panels, one change named.
2. *The restraint list* 4:5: serif headline, 3 numbered removals.
3. *Promise reel cover* 9:16: octopus, serif promise, centred.
**Motion.** Slow: teal line draws (2 s), blur-ins (1.4–1.6 s), serif fades up. Nothing bounces.
**Sound.** Music: luxe minimal, 70–86 BPM, harp, celesta, warm pad, upright piano. Sting: harp gliss up a Cmaj7 + one celesta ping (1.3 s), `sound/sting-12s.wav`. SFX: silk swish (transition), soft glass tick (UI), celesta ping (success). Licence: original; samples GeneralUser GS v2.0.3.
**Speech.** Optional: male, 40–55, calm British English, slow, low. AI voice only if disclosed on screen.
**CTA.** "Free guide in bio." One per post, never pushy.
**Outreach voice.** Polite, precise, generous. 1) Comment "My logo has 3 fonts 😅" → "Most do. Keep the one that reads best at small sizes and let the others go." 2) DM "Can you fix my logo?" → "Send it over with one line about your customers. I'll tell you the first thing I'd remove."
**Banned.** Rainbow gradients, drop shadows on text, stock "luxury" gold, any 3Shamrocks branding, the old shared network template.
