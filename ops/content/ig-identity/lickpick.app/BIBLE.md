# @lickpick.app · Instagram identity bible (v1, 17 Nov 2026)
Sources: `lickpick/index.html` + `lickpick/app/index.html` tokens, icons (`svg.i`) and pick logo · `ops/content/lickpick-30-keepers/BRAND-NOTES.md` · landing copy for every product claim.

**Audience.** Hobby and gigging guitar players (16–45) who film themselves and have hundreds of unposted jams. They live in guitar culture: licks, gear, practice, "my camera roll is full".
**Positioning.** Guitar lessons and jam culture from players who hit record a lot; LickPick finds your best lick in the jam and cuts the flubs.
**Voice** (English, guitar-friend, short).
- Do: teach one real lick or idea per post (correct theory, tab). · Do: talk like a bandmate ("Push it up."). · Do: say **licks**, never "takes".
- Don't: app-feature lists. · Don't: invented user numbers or results. · Don't: anything that isn't guitar.

**Colour roles.** Stage `#0B0912` · panel `#15111F` / raise `#1E1830` / line `#332A4A` · text `#FFF7F0` / dim `#C9BEDB` / mute `#8F83B3` · ink `#07060B` (outlines, text on volt) · **volt `#FFE600`** (stickers, KEEP, root notes, CTA) · **hot `#FF2E88`** (hard offset shadows, bends) · cyan `#2BE7FF` (waveform, step chips) · blaze `#B47CFF` (amp strip only) · amp strip = hot/blaze/volt/cyan hard bands.
**Type roles.** Display: Dela Gothic One, caps, 80–150 px, on volt stickers with 5 px ink border + 10 px hot shadow. Text: Archivo 700–800, 28–38 px.
**Grid look.** Loud and physical: real hands-on-guitar photos alternating with volt-sticker lesson cards. Amp strip on every tile.
**Imagery.** Hands and guitar only (Dave's real footage, cropped below the chest), phone-recording moments, fretboard and tab diagrams. Never a face. No stock guitarists. No old app screens (they say "takes").
**Icons.** The app's own: 2.6 px round stroke in volt + hot offset fill (`icons/`).
**Assets.** Stickers (volt, tilted ±2°), KEEP stamp, pick logo (top-left), note chips, waveform strip with the picked lick in cyan, step chips "Step 1 · the map".
**3 signature formats** (`samples/`).
1. *Hands cover* 4:5: photo top 75%, 2 stacked stickers, one dim line.
2. *Lick lesson card* 4:5: chip, volt headline with hot shadow, fretboard or tab, note chips, one plain sentence.
3. *KEEP reel cover* 9:16: hands photo, tilted KEEP stamp, waveform, 2-line payoff.
**Motion.** Amp-band wipes (4 bands, 0.35 s), sticker slams (scale 1.8→1, 0.28 s), pick drops with a bounce, waveform bars pulse to the beat. Cuts on the downbeat.
**Sound.** Music: blues-rock / indie rock, 100–124 BPM, the player's own guitar first; overdriven guitar, finger bass, live drums. Sting: power hit + crash, then the A-minor lick (1.5 s), `sound/sting-12s.wav`. SFX: pick scrape (transition), string tick (UI), KEEP = crash + snare hit (success). Licence: original; samples GeneralUser GS v2.0.3; never a copyrighted song or riff.
**Speech.** Optional; male or female 20s–30s, casual US/UK English, fast. "Push it up a whole step. Check it against the B string."
**CTA.** "Link in bio" sticker; "Free on Android + Chrome" (landing copy); comment prompts about the lick ("What would you play next?").
**Outreach voice.** Bandmate tone, emoji-light. 1) Comment "Mine sounds out of tune" → "Check the bend against the B string, 5th fret. If it matches, you're there 🎸" 2) DM "Is it on iPhone?" → "Right now it's free on Android and in Chrome. iPhone is coming; want a ping when it lands?"
**Banned.** Dave's face · the word "takes" · text-only empty cards · screenshots of old screens · music we don't own · fake reviews or download counts.
