The problem we're solving

Our parents are living longer but too often alone.

Modern life scatters families across cities and time zones. Adult children carry guilt. Elders carry silence. The care gap is real, it is growing, and it is a public health crisis we can no longer ignore.

🤐

Silence is dangerous

Cognitive decline, depression, and health emergencies often go undetected because there is simply no one checking in daily. A missed medication or an unnoticed fall can escalate in hours.

📵

Existing tech is alienating

Smart speakers feel cold. Medical alert buttons carry stigma. Video calls require scheduling. Elders need something that meets them where they are — conversational, warm, and frictionless.

😰

Families carry invisible weight

Adult children spend their days worrying: Did Dad take his pills? Is Mom eating? Did she fall? Professional care is expensive and impersonal. The middle-of-the-night anxiety is constant.

🏥

Care facilities can't fill the gap

Most elders want to age in place — their own home, their own pace. Facilities are expensive, often impersonal, and frequently the last resort. Home is where Ezra belongs.

Meet the companion

Not a robot. Not an app. A presence.

"Good morning, Miriam. How did you sleep? I remembered your granddaughter's recital is tomorrow — shall we think about what to wear?"

Ezra learns the person — their rhythms, their stories, their names and dates. It speaks with warmth, remembers context across conversations, and gently watches for signs that something might be off. It is not clinical. It is not cold. It is a companion designed to feel like a trusted presence in the room.

Why "Ezra"?

Ezra is a biblical Hebrew name meaning "helper" or "aid." It carries dignity, warmth, and the weight of thousands of years of care. A name an elder would trust, and a grandchild would remember.

Life Stories

Their stories deserve to live forever.

Every elder carries a lifetime of stories that will vanish with them. Recipes. Migrations. Wars survived. Love found. Lessons learned. Ezra turns ordinary conversations into a living archive that grandchildren will treasure for generations.

  • 🎙️
    Passive capture

    Stories emerge naturally through conversation — no formal "recording session" that feels clinical or awkward.

  • 📝
    AI transcription & organization

    Stories are transcribed, tagged by theme and date, and organized into a searchable family memoir.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Sharable family archive

    Every family member gets access. Grandchildren can search for stories about their names, read them, and hear their elder's voice.

  • 📚
    Heritage preservation

    Recipes, traditions, prayers in original languages — Ezra captures the cultural texture of a life, not just the facts.

Privacy first

Your family's data is yours.

We are building something for the most vulnerable people in society. That demands the highest standard of privacy and trust — not as a feature, as a foundation.

🔐

End-to-end encrypted

All conversations, health data, and family records are encrypted in transit and at rest. Only your family holds the keys.

🚫

No advertising. Ever.

We will never monetize elder health data through ads or sell it to third parties. Full stop. Our revenue model is subscription only.

🏠

Data stays in your region

Family data is stored in the same jurisdiction as the family. We support HIPAA, GDPR, and Israeli privacy law compliance from day one.

🎛️

Full family control

The elder and designated family members control every permission. Any family member can delete all data at any time, permanently.

👁️

Transparent listening

Ezra only listens when spoken to. No passive surveillance. A clear visual indicator shows when the mic is active — always.

🔍

Audited by design

Third-party privacy audits are built into our roadmap before launch. We will publish the results publicly.
