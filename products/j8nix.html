The problem

Running many projects at once is a memory game you lose.

Status lives in your head, in six tabs, and in a notebook you cannot find. The thing that is actually blocking a launch gets buried under everything that is not.

  • x You reopen five tools just to remember where one project stands.
  • x The task that unblocks everything is lost in a pile of small ones.
  • x "What was I about to do?" costs you the first twenty minutes of every day.
  • x Progress is real, but there is no single place that shows it.

J8N.IX fixes the view.

It pulls every project into one board, sorts by what is live and what is stalled, and surfaces the one next action per project so you always know where to start. No digging, no guessing, no cold-start mornings.
