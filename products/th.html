The problem

Everyone walks past it.

You see the bottle on the beach. The wrapper in the park. The pile at the corner. You think someone should do something, and then you keep walking. We all do.

Not because people are bad. Because nothing about picking it up feels like it counts.

Trashure-Hunters makes it count. Every piece of real trash you clean becomes XP, a new Sproutz, and a step your whole clan can see.

Three steps. Then the planet is cleaner.

No points for pretending. The game is built so the only way to win is to actually clean something real.
