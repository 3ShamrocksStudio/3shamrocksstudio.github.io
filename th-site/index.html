The Sky RealmRealm 2 of 6

How the hunt works

The game loop is a real cleanup. The app just makes it a quest.

  1. Spot it

    Find litter on the live map's Grime Zones, or anywhere you walk with a grown-up.

  2. Clean it

    Pick it up safely, with gloves or a grabber, and put it in the right bin.

  3. Prove it

    Snap a before and after with Trash-Cam. It checks for trash, not people.

  4. Heal the realm

    Earn XP, clear the zone, and watch your Elemonster grow from seed or egg towards Realm Keeper.

Wispo guides this realm. The jokester, always on the move. He makes the first cleanup feel like play.

Our safety promises

These are the rules the game is being built to, for parents and teachers to hold us to.

Blazo guards this realmThe hothead with a heart. Fiercely loyal to the people he protects.

A parent says yes first

Players under 13 need a parent or guardian's consent before the camera or location is switched on.

The camera looks for trash

Trash-Cam checks the litter in the photo, not faces. Photos are deleted once the cleanup is verified.

No exact locations shared

Location places Grime Zones on the map. Other players never see where a child is.

No chat with strangers

There is no in-game chat. Teammates appear as avatars only.

No ads aimed at kids

We won't show advertising to children, and we won't sell or rent anyone's data.

No tricks to keep them hooked

No pay-to-win, no countdown pressure and no loot boxes. Progress comes from cleaning.

The full privacy policy is being written now and will be published before any family can join the beta. Questions in the meantime: dave@3shamrocks.com.

Out in the real world, the game teaches these first

  • Hunt with a grown-up nearby
  • Wear gloves or use a grabber
  • Never touch glass, needles or anything sharp
  • Stay away from roads, water edges and private property
  • If in doubt, leave it and tell an adult
  • Wash your hands when you're done
