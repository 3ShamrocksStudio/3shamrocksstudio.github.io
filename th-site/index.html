The River of Three

Follow the river to the light

One living current runs through all six realms, from the Cosmic Void all the way to the Temple of Light. Scroll down and travel it.

Five young friends

Sproutz, Bloop, Blazo, Wispo and Cosmix grew up together in five different realms, joined by the River of Three, the living current that flows through every world.

Cosmix guides this realm. The wise one. She mapped the way back to the light before anyone else was brave enough to try.

2The Sky Realm Reached at 500 XP

Meet Orbo

At the centre of every world glows Orbo, the ancient keeper of light. Together with the Realm Keepers he creates Compos: gentle creatures that keep forests green, oceans blue and stars bright.

Wispo guides this realm. The jokester, always on the move. He makes the first cleanup feel like play.

3The Deep Waters Reached at 2,500 XP

People forgot to look down

In our world, trash began to pile up. It slipped into creeks and rivers and drifted all the way down the River of Three. Far away, Orbo grew tired, fighting the grime all alone.

Bloop guides this realm. The empath. Feels every bit of grime in the water, and every bit you clear.

4The Green Lands Reached at 5,000 XP

King Grime awakes!

The five friends sneaked into the Temple and stood in a circle around Orbo. Their power hit the grime inside him, and King Grime burst out. The Grime Signal blasted through every realm.

Sproutz guides this realm. The gentle giant who leads with love. The Green Lands are his home, and the hardest place to see dimmed.

Blazo guards this realm. The hothead with a heart. Fiercely loyal to the people he protects.

The Hunter's Code

Our safety promises

These are the rules the game is being built to, for parents and teachers to hold us to.

👨‍👩‍👧

A parent says yes first

Players under 13 need a parent or guardian's consent before the camera or location is switched on.

📸

The camera looks for trash

Trash-Cam checks the litter in the photo, not faces. Photos are deleted once the cleanup is verified.

📍

No exact locations shared

Location places Grime Zones on the map. Other players never see where a child is.

🤐

No chat with strangers

There is no in-game chat. Teammates appear as avatars only.

🚫

No ads aimed at kids

We won't show advertising to children, and we won't sell or rent anyone's data.

🎯

No tricks to keep them hooked

No pay-to-win, no countdown pressure and no loot boxes. Progress comes from cleaning.

Out in the real world, the game teaches these first

  • Hunt with a grown-up nearby
  • Wear gloves or use a grabber
  • Never touch glass, needles or anything sharp
  • Stay away from roads, water edges and private property
  • If in doubt, leave it and tell an adult
  • Wash your hands when you're done
