Listing copy prompts

Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or whatever you use. Fill the [brackets] with real facts only.

Before you paste anything
  • Fact-check every line the AI writes against the listing. It will invent details if you let it.
  • Describe the property, not the people who “should” live there. Fair housing rules apply to ad copy.
  • Check your MLS's character limits and remarks rules before you publish.

MLS public remarks

MLS
You are an experienced listing copywriter. Write MLS public remarks for the property below.

Rules:
- Use only the facts I give you. Do not invent features, upgrades, views or distances.
- Describe the property, not the ideal buyer (no "perfect for families", "great for retirees"). Follow US fair housing advertising guidelines.
- Lead with the single strongest feature. Plain, specific words. No clichés like "must see", "won't last", "nestled".
- Maximum [character limit] characters including spaces.

Facts:
- Area / neighborhood: [ ]
- Beds / baths / sq ft: [ ]
- Lot and outdoor space: [ ]
- Updates, with years: [ ]
- Standout features: [ ]
- Nearby (only verifiable names, e.g. a park or transit line): [ ]

Give me two versions: one warm, one crisp.

Headline + feature bullets

Flyer
From the facts below, write:
1) Three headline options, max 8 words each, for a listing flyer and portal.
2) Six feature bullets, max 10 words each, most important first.
Use only these facts. No exaggeration, no claims about who the home suits.

Facts: [paste your listing facts]

Just-listed social caption

Social

- First line: the most striking real detail of the home (no "Just listed!" opener).
- 3 short lines on the best features, from the facts below only.
- One clear next step: [DM me / link in bio / call].
- End with the brokerage line: [Brokerage name, license info as required].
- Max 5 relevant hashtags.

Open-house announcement

Post

Highlight 2 real features: [feature 1], [feature 2].

Seller update emails

Sellers stay calm when they hear from you before they have to ask.

Weekly seller update

Email
Write a short weekly update email from me to my sellers.
Include, in this order:
- Showings this week: [number]
- Online activity, if I have it: [views / saves from the portal]
- Buyer feedback themes: [paste notes]
- What I did this week: [ ]
- What I'll do next week: [ ]
Tone: warm, factual, confident. Under 180 words. End by offering a quick call and give two time options: [ ], [ ].

The price conversation

Email
Help me write a calm, respectful email to my sellers about how the market has responded so far.
Facts:
- Days on market: [ ]
- Showings: [ ]  Second showings: [ ]
- Feedback themes: [ ]
- Recent comparable sales: [address, price, date] x3
Lay out three options without pushing: hold as is, refresh (photos, copy, staging), or adjust the price. Explain the trade-off of each in one or two sentences. Ask for a call to decide together. Under 220 words.
04

Speed-to-lead replies

Reply while they're still looking at the listing. Short, specific, one question.

1 · Inquiry about a listing

Buyer
Hi [first name], it's [Your name] with [Brokerage]. Got your question about [address]. It's [still available / under contract].
Want to see it? I have [day, time] or [day, time].
Reply STOP to opt out.

2 · After hours

Buyer
Hi [first name], [Your name] with [Brokerage]. Thanks for your message about [address]. I'll reply properly first thing tomorrow. If you'd like a tour, send me two times that work for you.

3 · Just browsing

Buyer
No pressure at all, [first name]. What would make a home a yes for you: area, price range, must-haves? I'll send you matches as they come up, nothing else.

4 · What's my home worth?

Seller
Thanks for reaching out about [address], [first name]. Online estimates can be off in either direction, so I'd rather give you a real range.
Can I ask three quick questions about updates and condition? Or I can stop by for 15 minutes on [day].

5 · Next-day follow-up

Follow-up
Hi [first name], [Your name] again. Still curious about [address / homes in area]? Happy to send similar places or set up a quick tour. Just reply here.

6 · Voicemail

Call
Hi [first name], it's [Your name] with [Brokerage], getting back to you about [address]. I'm texting you too so you have my number. Talk soon.
Texting leads

Text people who reached out to you, include your name and brokerage, and stop when anyone asks. Your brokerage and state may have extra rules on texting and calling leads: check them.
