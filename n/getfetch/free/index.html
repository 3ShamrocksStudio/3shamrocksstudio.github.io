01

Text-back scripts

Swap the [brackets] for your details. Keep each text short: your business name, one question, one next step.

1 · Missed call, any trade

General
Hi, this is [Name] at [Business]. Sorry we missed your call, we're out on a job. What can we help with? Reply here and we'll get back to you by [time].
Reply STOP to opt out.

2 · HVAC: no heat / no cool

HVAC
Hi, it's [Name] with [Business]. Sorry we missed you. Is your system not heating or cooling, or is this about maintenance?
Text your address and a quick description and we'll reply with the next open slot.
If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas company or 911 from outside.

3 · HVAC: tune-up or quote

HVAC
Thanks for calling [Business]! Looking for a tune-up or a quote on a new system? Reply with your address and the best day for a visit, and we'll confirm a time window.

4 · Plumbing: leak or emergency

Plumbing
Hi, [Name] from [Business] here. Sorry we missed your call. If water is leaking right now, turn off the main water valve if you can do it safely.
Then reply LEAK with your address and we'll call you back as soon as we can.

5 · Med spa: booking

Med spa
Hi, it's [Name] at [Spa]. Sorry we missed your call! Are you looking to book a treatment or ask a question?
You can book here: [booking link]. Or reply with a day that works and we'll find you a time.

6 · After hours

After hours
Thanks for calling [Business]. We're closed right now and back at [time] on [day].
Reply with what you need and your address, and we'll text you first thing.
[Emergency line or instruction, if you offer one]

7 · Next-day follow-up (no reply)

Follow-up
Hi, [Name] from [Business] again. Just checking you got sorted. If you still need a hand, reply with a good time to call. No reply needed if you're all set.

8 · Booked confirmation

Confirm
You're booked with [Business]: [day], between [time window]. [Tech name] will text when on the way.
Need to change it? Just reply here.
Med spas: keep health details off text

Ask what they want to book, not about medical history. Take health questions on the phone or through a secure intake form.

2-minute call-log audit

Open your phone or VoIP app's call history and filter to the last 7 days. Count the numbers below. The page does the math; nothing you type leaves your browser.

What the numbers tell you
  • Most misses during working hours? It's a routing problem: a second phone, a call-forward rule, or an instant text-back.
  • Most misses after hours? Use the after-hours script (6) on autopilot.
  • D far below B? Your fix is follow-up, not more ads.
  • Run this again in 30 days with the same steps and compare.
03

A simple follow-up rhythm

Three touches, then stop. If anyone replies STOP, stop everything.

  1. Within a minuteAutomatic text-backScript 1 in hours, script 6 after hours. The caller knows they reached a real business.
  2. Within 15 minutes, in hoursCall back onceIf they don't pick up, keep the voicemail short. Your text is already in their phone.
  3. Next morningOne follow-up textScript 7. Friendly, no pressure.
  4. Day 3Last check-in, then stop“Still need help with [issue]? Reply anytime and we'll get you booked.”
  5. After the jobThank-you + review requestOnly to customers you actually served.
Texting rules (US): the short version

Put your business name in every text, honor STOP right away, and don't send marketing texts to people who haven't agreed to receive them. If your texting tool sends from a regular 10-digit local number, US carriers require the business to register for A2P 10DLC; most tools walk you through it. More at The Campaign Registry.
