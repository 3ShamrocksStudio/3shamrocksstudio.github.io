The script template

Hook, body, CTA. One idea per video, one visual per beat.

Script template

Template
HOOK (first 1–2 seconds, said AND on screen)
[A question, a surprising true fact, or "Stop doing X"]

SETUP (one line: why the viewer should care)
[ ]

BODY (3 beats max, one visual each)
1. [ ]
2. [ ]
3. [ ]

PAYOFF (the answer or the result)
[ ]

CTA (one only)
[Follow for part 2 / Save this for later / Comment your answer]

Notes: on-screen text max ~6 words per card · captions on · target length [15–45 s]

Example · History

Example
HOOK: "OK" started as a joke.
SETUP: An 1830s newspaper fad.
BODY: 1. Old newspaper page, 1839 Boston. 2. On-screen: the fad of jokey abbreviations, "O.K." = "oll korrect". 3. 1840 campaign poster: "Old Kinderhook" kept it alive.
PAYOFF: A misspelled joke became a word used around the world.
CTA: Follow for more word stories.
(Check the story against a reliable source before you post.)

04

AI disclosure

Major platforms ask creators to label realistic content that was made or changed with AI. Settings move around, so check each platform's help page before you post.

  1. YouTubeAsks you to disclose realistic altered or synthetic content when you upload. YouTube Help
  2. TikTokAsks you to label realistic AI-generated content, using its AI-generated content setting when you post. TikTok Help
  3. Instagram & FacebookMeta can add an “AI info” label, and asks you to label photorealistic video or realistic audio made or altered with AI. Check Meta's Help Center for the current steps.
When in doubt, label it

Say it in the caption too, and never use AI to impersonate a real person or fake an endorsement.
