02

Prompt templates

Text prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Image prompts work in most image generators; results vary by tool, so run each a few times.

Two honest limits
  • Image models still struggle with exact lettering. Use them to explore marks and moods, then set the final wordmark in a real typeface in a vector tool.
  • Run a trademark search on the name and the mark before you commit.

1 · Brief → positioning

Text
Here is my brand brief:
[paste your brief]

Turn it into:
- A positioning line, max 12 words.
- 3 personality traits, each with a "not this" opposite.
- The audience in one sentence.
- 3 distinct visual directions, each with a short name, a mood in 5 words, and what it would look like.
Be specific. No buzzwords.

2 · Logo symbol

Image
Minimal logo symbol for [brand name], a [what you do] for [audience]. Concept: [one idea, e.g. "a key that is also a leaf"]. Flat vector style, single color [HEX] on a plain white background, bold simple geometry, readable at 16px, no text, no gradient, no shadow, centered with generous padding.

3 · Monogram

Image
Monogram logo using the letters [XY] for [brand name], feeling [3 words]. Geometric, single stroke weight, one color [HEX] on white, flat vector style, no other text, high contrast, centered.

4 · Wordmark direction

Image
Wordmark style exploration for the name "[brand name]": [serif / sans / rounded / condensed], feeling [3 words]. Black on white, flat, no symbol. Show 4 variations side by side.
(Use as a reference only. Rebuild the final in a real font.)

5 · Palette from personality

Text
Suggest 3 color palettes for a brand that feels [3 words] and never [3 words].
Each palette: 1 dark neutral, 1 light neutral, 1 primary, 1 accent, with HEX codes.
For each, name which pairs you'd use for body text, and explain the palette in one line.
Keep accents to one per palette.

6 · Moodboard

Image
Moodboard, 3x3 grid, for a [industry] brand that feels [3 words]: textures, materials, lighting, photography style, objects from the customer's world. Colors limited to [HEX, HEX, HEX]. Editorial, natural light, no text, no logos.

7 · Type pairing

Text
Recommend 3 font pairings (headline + body) that are free on Google Fonts, for a brand that feels [3 words]. For each: why it fits, which weights to use, and one mistake to avoid. Prefer typefaces with good legibility at small sizes.

8 · Brand in the real world

Image
Realistic product photo of a [business card / shopping bag / storefront sign / app icon on a phone] for a brand called "[brand name]". Minimal design using only [HEX, HEX], lots of empty space, soft daylight, real materials, no extra text.

9 · Strict critique

Text + image
Act as a strict brand design director. Critique the attached logo against these criteria, 1–5 each:
1) Readable at 16px  2) Works in one color  3) Clear at a glance  4) Different from [competitors]  5) Matches [3 words].
Then list the 3 changes with the biggest impact, most important first. Be direct.

10 · Voice lines

Text
Write 10 tagline options for [brand name], who [what you do] for [audience].
Rules: under 7 words, plain language, no puns, no "elevate", "unlock" or "seamless". Match this personality: [3 words].
Mark your top 2 and say why.
