Cool, low-saturation colours lower arousal, so breathe and check-in screens stay indigo and ice. Warm colours read as energy and reward, so pink and coral appear only when a child finishes something. Never use warm colours on the crisis or grown-up help screens.

Icon style

  • Pixel icons on a 12 × 12 grid, square pixels, no anti-aliasing.
  • One colour per icon. Fur on dark, Ink on light. The state colour shows only when active.
  • Icons sit in a rounded tile (22% radius), like the app icon.
  • No outline icons, no gradients, no emoji.

Character rules (shades-free)

  • No sunglasses, ever. His eyes carry the mood.
  • Same bear, same pose, same pixel size (about 9 px cells at 1408 px). Edit the eyes and mouth; never redraw him.
  • Eyes: deep ink with a 2 × 2 white highlight top-left and a small ice glint. Keep the highlight in every open-eye state.
  • The popsicle stays. It means "cool down".
  • Milo lives on Night. On light pages he sits in his Night tile; never cut him out onto white.
  • Scale with nearest-neighbour at whole steps (2×, 3×). Never blur or smooth the pixels.
  • Never: a phone, money, weapons or a screen in his paws, or text in his speech.
