# Rookie & King · Brand v5

> **Proposal, not live.** These files live in `assets/brand-proposals/rk/`; the site still loads the approved v4 kit in `assets/brand-v3/rk/`. NEEDS YOUR EYES.

**Idea:** one piece, two roles. The mark is a single chess piece split down the middle: the left half is a pawn (the Rookie, ivory), the right half is a king (brass). At the board, both are the same piece. That is the product promise: anyone can meet anyone as an equal for ten minutes.

v5 replaces the v4 heritage crest (shield, laurel, ribbon). The crest read as a sports badge. v5 is built for a B2B networking product: flat, geometric, quiet, one accent colour.

## Mark rules
- Two halves, one gap. Keep the gap (9 units on the 512 grid). Never close it, never swap the colours (pawn left, king right).
- One-colour version: the pawn half at 42% opacity of the same colour (see brand card).
- Minimum size: 16 px tall for the mark, 24 px for the app tile.

## Wordmark
**Rookie & King** in Inter Display SemiBold, tracking -3.5%. The ampersand alone is Instrument Serif Italic in Brass. That single serif glyph is the "game" edge in an otherwise strict B2B system.

## Type
- Display: **Inter Display SemiBold** (-3.5% tracking at large sizes).
- Accent: **Instrument Serif Italic**, one glyph or one word per layout.
- Interface: **Inter** Medium / SemiBold.

## Voice
A calm club host. "Your move." "Good game. Shake hands?" Never "crush", "dominate" or "win the room".

## Icons
24 / 48 grid, 2.75 stroke, round caps. Ivory line + one Brass accent per icon: Challenge, 10-min game, Board, Handshake, Meeting booked, Lounge, Leaderboard, Outcomes.
