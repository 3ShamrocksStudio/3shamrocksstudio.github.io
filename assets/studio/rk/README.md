# Rookie & King: brand kit (studio portfolio)

**Brand idea:** a rookie and a king share one base. At the board, anyone can meet anyone as an equal.

## Files
- `logo.svg` / `logo.png` (800 px, transparent): stacked lockup, mark above wordmark. Built for dark navy backgrounds.
- `mark.svg` / `mark.png` (512 px, transparent corners): app-icon tile, a gold pawn and an ivory king on one shared gold plinth.
- `scr-1.webp` Challenge, `scr-2.webp` Game, `scr-3.webp` After the game (1179 x 2556, iPhone 15 Pro).

The wordmark is converted to outlines in the SVG, so it needs no font files.

## Type
- Headings and wordmark: **Playfair Display** 600, with the italic `&` in gold.
- UI: **Inter** 400–700.

## Notes
- All names, companies and the `rookieandking.app` URL in the screens are invented demo content.
- No third-party logos are used. The network is described as "your network".
- The board shows a legal Giuoco Piano position after 7…Bg4, with White to move.
