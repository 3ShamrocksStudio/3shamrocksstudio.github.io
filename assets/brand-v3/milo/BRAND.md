# Milo · Brand v3

**Idea:** Milo is a bear who's got you. Cool on the outside, soft on the inside.

## Direction and rationale
- Dave preferred an earlier **bear in sunglasses** over the round sprout mascot. We searched the studio repo's git history, Milo's own git history (including the deleted `Brand/logos/milo-a.jpg` and `milo-b.jpg`), the Mac folders and past chats. That bear was not in any of them, so this one is **a new original design** based on his description.
- **Why sunglasses work for a kids' feelings companion:** the shades give Milo confidence and a bit of swagger, so a child feels backed up by a cool friend rather than pitied. The face under the shades stays soft: rounded muzzle, blush cheeks, a gentle "w" smile.
- **The shades are the expression system.** On = Cool (default), pushed up = Proud (eyes smiling), slid down = Curious (eyes peeking). One asset, many moods, no new character art needed per state. This matches the app's mood mirroring.
- It continues the existing Milo palette (terracotta hero, honey heart, cocoa ink, oat world), so the app's warm look carries over. No alarming reds, and no dark green theme.

## Palette
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Terracotta | `#E2673C` | Hero, fur |
| Honey | `#E9A23B` | Heart, rewards, highlights |
| Oat | `#FBF3E8` | Canvas |
| Cocoa | `#3A281F` | Ink, wordmark, shades |
| Blush | `#F49A84` | Cheeks, soft states |
| Sprout | `#6FA96F` | Growth accent, small use only |

## Type
- Wordmark and display: **Fredoka 700**, lowercase `milo`, with the dot of the i replaced by a honey heart.
- Body: **Nunito 600–800**.

## Files
- `logo.png`: lockup for light backgrounds (primary). `logo-on-dark.png`: lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `logo-mark.png` / `mark.svg`: the bear (Cool), transparent, 1024 px.
- `mark-happy.svg` (Proud), `mark-peek.svg` (Curious), `expressions.png`: the expression set.
- `app-icon.png`: bear on an oat tile.
- `brand-card.png`: 1200×1200 portfolio card.

## Do
- Lead with the Cool bear. Switch to Proud after a care moment and to Curious when Milo asks something.
- Keep the heart honey, on the i only. It is the one heart in the system.
- Use oat and cream grounds.

## Don't
- Don't take the shades off completely. They're Milo's signature.
- Don't use Milo on dark green or red. Crisis states use honey and amber, never red.
- Don't add mirrored or reflective "eyes" to the lenses. The eyes are only shown when the shades move.
