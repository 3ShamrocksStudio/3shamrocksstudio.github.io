# Chore Royale · Brand v4 (icons v6, 12 Nov 2026)

**Idea:** the family is a royal court at war over chores. Spin the Wheel of Choretune, grab, steal, swap and duel, let the parent seal it, take the crown.
**Product core:** the war moves (grab, steal, shield, swap, duel, bounty, 2X, parent seal, weekly crown) are in the staging app. The Wheel of Choretune is designed, not built.
**Name:** always **Chore Royale**.

## Source
Dave's final logo, `Chore-Wars/Brand/logos/Chore-Royale_FinalLogo.jpg`: a crowned purple knight with a giant spoon, above a gold-framed purple plaque with gems, with chunky gold "CHORE ROYALE" type. v4 redraws that logo as clean vectors. No AI pixels ship.

## Logo system
- **Mark** (`logo-mark.png`, `mark.svg`): the crowned knight's helm. Purple helm in a gold rim, T-visor, amethyst gem on the brow, three-point gold crown, the silver Spoon-Sceptre behind, a teal spell-glow, bubbles and sparkles.
- **Stacked logo** (`logo-stacked.png`): the helm rising out of the gem-studded plaque with CHORE / ROYALE. This is the final logo, rebuilt.
- **Horizontal** (`logo.png`) and **wordmark** (`wordmark.png`): one line of gold Bevan with a Royal Ink outline and a purple drop.
- **App icon** (`app-icon.png`): the helm on a spotlit purple tile.
- **Hero** (`hero.png`): the court with Pass, XP and Crowns cards.

## Icons (v6)
Files: `assets/work/chore-royale/icons-v6.svg` (26 symbols, ids `cr-*`), `icons-v6-board.png` (board), `icons-v6-sheet.png` (32 / 64 / 128 px contact sheet). v6 replaces the v4 set (`icons.svg`) and v5.

Set: Wheel of Choretune, Duel (sword vs Spoon-Sceptre), Steal (magnet), Swap, Shield, Bounty, Crown, Crowns coin, XP, Momentum flame, 2X card, Photo proof, Parent seal, Jackpot, Skip token, Mystery, Freeze, Season, Quest, and the chores: Sweep, Trash out, Dishes, Laundry, Make bed, Feed pet, Bathroom.

Rules:
1. Royal Ink `#14072E` outline, 4.6 on the 64 grid (2.3 px at 32 px), round joins. Small inner parts use a thinner outline (1.6 to 3).
2. One light, from the top. Every solid shape has a pale rim on top, a deep rim below, and a top-to-bottom gradient body.
3. A solid ink drop 3.2 under the whole icon. It reads as a chunky game sticker.
4. Fills come only from the palette: Crown Gold, team purple (follows the banner colour), Amethyst, Spell Teal, Battle Rose, Spoon Silver, Suds, plus Amber for wood.
5. One gloss highlight, top-left. Never more.
6. Every icon must read at 32 px on purple, Night Court and Suds. Check the contact sheet before shipping a new one.
7. No text inside icons, except the 2X card.
8. 100% original shapes. Game-quality polish is the bar, but nothing is copied from other games.
