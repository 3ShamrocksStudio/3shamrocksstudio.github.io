FaiRent · brand system v5 · 12 Nov 2026

The renter’s red pen.

FaiRent is renters for renters. Everyone puts their real rent on paper, and together they mark it up: strike the overcharge, highlight the fair price, pass the pen to the next renter.

the whole brand, in 3 marks:
  • StrikeRed Pen through a rent above the fair band. Only then.
  • HighlightHighlighter behind the fair number. The thing to remember.
  • Cut at 21°Every slash, chip and stamp leans the same way.
01 · Logo system

Logo and mark

Hand-built from Rubik 800 outlines. The F’s top bar ends in a 21° cut, and the off-cut, in Red Pen, is the money the renter keeps. The same chip dots the i in the wordmark.

05 · Motion

Four moves, one angle

Fast in, settle with a small overshoot. Nothing floats or loops in the product: each move answers something the renter did.

The cut

The off-cut drops: the price comes down. Logo intro, success.

The strike

₪8,400

Red Pen through an asking rent above the fair band. 450 ms, ease-in.

The swipe

₪7,346

Highlighter behind the fair number, left to right at 21°.

The stamp

+14%A bit above

The verdict lands at −7° with a snap. Red when over, ink when fair.

Easing: --fr-snap cubic-bezier(.3,1.5,.5,1) for landings, --fr-drop cubic-bezier(.5,0,.9,.5) for strikes. Page transition: two Highlighter halves meet on a red seam. All motion stops under reduced motion.

06 · Voice

A friend with the numbers

Calm, on the renter’s side, never angry at landlords. Every number names its source. A shared benchmark, never an appraisal.

Say

“Renters in Tel Aviv–Yafo pay about ₪6,976 for 2.5–3 rooms (CBS, Q2 2025).”

Not

“Your landlord is ripping you off!”

Say

“Is there room to set the rent at ₪7,350?”

Not

“Guaranteed savings on every lease.”

Brand line, from the hand-lettered original: Stop paying more for less.
