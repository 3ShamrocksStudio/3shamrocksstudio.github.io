# R&K (Rookie & King) · Brand v6

**Idea:** a rookie and a king meet on one board. Professional connection first, played as a game. Audience: business professionals. Status: concept, not built yet.

## The mark
- One chess piece split in two: the **Rookie** (pawn head, Signal Blue) and the **King** (crown, Victory Amber).
- **Puzzle seam:** the halves lock together like puzzle pieces. A blue tab reaches into the King's body, an amber tab reaches back into the Rookie's head. Two sides, one deal.
- v6 removes the white collar bar, flattens the finish (subtle vertical gradient only) and simplifies the crown to three clean points.
- Files: `logo-mark.png` (transparent), `mark.svg`, `app-icon.png` (full-bleed square; the platform applies its own mask).

## Lockups
- `logo.png`: for dark backgrounds (white wordmark, Sky Blue tagline).
- `logo-on-light.png`: for light backgrounds (Ink wordmark, Amber Deep ampersand, Signal Blue tagline).
- Tagline: **PLAY THE GAME. BUILD THE NETWORK.**

## Palette and meaning
Cool and neutral colours carry cold, waiting and status. Warm amber is kept for the win: the move, the handshake, the meeting, the connection.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#070D1C` | Base canvas |
| Board Navy | `#0F1E3F` | Surfaces, cards |
| Signal Blue | `#1F5BFF` | Rookie half, primary actions (white text 5.3:1) |
| Sky Blue | `#5A97FF` | Links, info, move indicators (6.7:1 on Ink) |
| Steel | `#8B9AB8` | Cold and neutral: cold outreach, status, labels (6.8:1 on Ink) |
| Victory Amber | `#F59E0B` | King half, the win, success actions (9.0:1 on Ink; Ink text on it 9.0:1) |
| Amber Deep | `#B45309` | Amber on light backgrounds (5.0:1 on white) |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark |

## Type
- Display: **Inter Tight 700–800** (Google Fonts). Sentence case for headlines, caps only for the wordmark.
- Interface: **Inter 400–600** (Google Fonts). Tabular numerals for clocks.
