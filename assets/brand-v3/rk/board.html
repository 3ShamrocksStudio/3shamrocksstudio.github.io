Logo system

His working board held four drafts. The system uses all four, each with one job.

Why these choices

  • Mark = rook + king. His rook half and king half, cut from one silhouette and split by a single line: the Rookie (ROOKIE starts with a rook) and the King, one piece. Connection and business, not "versus". Two draft artifacts were cleaned with his own paths only: the tapering seam is now one constant-width split line (his gap width), and a hairline slit in the king's base was closed along its outer edge.
  • Wordmark = the infinity OO. The double O of ROOKIE becomes one loop: the network that keeps going after the game. It's legible at every size, so it carries the primary lockup.
  • Display wordmark = rook-I and king-I. The I in ROOKIE is a rook, the I in KING is a king, echoing the mark. Its cut-in pieces get thin when small, so it's for heroes, posters and splash screens at 320 px and up.
  • Alternate = pawn + king. His second split-piece draft. It's kept for illustration and the game's pawn, never as the logo.
  • Night background. Logos sit on Night #020617, a near-black navy, so Electric Blue and White pop (White 20:1).
  • Game pieces. The rook half and king half of the mark, each mirrored on its own axis, plus his pawn half: one family, rendered in blue lacquer and pearl.

Cool = cold, waiting, status

Steel and Mist carry the cold message, the pending invite, labels and clocks. Blue carries the action.

Warm = the win

Win Amber is the complement of his blue, so a small amount glows hard against Navy (8.3:1). It's used only for the handshake, the connection and the level-up, under 5% of any screen, and never in the logo.

Contrast (WCAG 2.x, measured): White on Navy 17.9 · White on Electric Blue 5.25 (buttons pass AA) · Electric Blue on Navy 3.4 (large type and UI only; links use Sky, 5.4) · Steel on Navy 6.3 · Amber on Navy 8.3 · Navy on Paper 16.6 · Electric Blue on Paper 4.9.

Tone rules

  • Professional first, game second. A sharp operator who plays chess, not a gamer.
  • Short and declarative. Verbs first.
  • One chess word per line at most (move, board, opening, endgame).
  • Respect rank, then remove it. Never mock rookies, never flatter kings.
  • Real results only. No invented members, stats or reviews.
  • Cold words stay cool (waiting, no reply). Warm words are for the win (connected, good game).
  • No cartoon voice, no exclamation stacks, no emoji in product UI.

Board spec

  • Frame: brushed slate under dark glass (Night #020617 to #0A1440), 24 radius, an electric-blue edge light that is brightest at the top-left and bottom-right corners.
  • Squares: light #14245A, dark #060C28, a 1 px Ice grid at 7%, a glass sheen from the top left.
  • Coordinates: Poppins 600, Ice at 50%.
  • Move hint: a glowing Ice dot in a blue ring. Last move: Electric Blue at 16%.
  • Moves: a blurred blue glow under a Sky-to-Ice core line, with lit nodes on every square passed.
  • Win: path, nodes and edge light turn amber, the only amber on the board.

Cards and surfaces

  • Canvas Night #020617; cards Surface #0B1D55, radius 16, 1 px line at Ice 14%.
  • Kicker label: Inter 600 caps in Steel, with the split chip .
  • Win state: amber border at 55% plus a soft amber glow, never an amber fill on cards.
  • Light sections: Paper #F4F6FB, Navy text, Amber Deep for warm accents.
