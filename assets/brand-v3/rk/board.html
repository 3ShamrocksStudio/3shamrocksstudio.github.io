Logo system

His working board held four drafts. The system uses all four, each with one job.

Why these choices

  • Mark = the split piece. A blue Rookie (pawn) and a white King, one silhouette split by a single line. Two people, one piece: connection and business, not “versus”. Its two colours are the wordmark's two colours.
  • Wordmark = the infinity OO. The double O of ROOKIE becomes one loop: the network that keeps going after the game. It is legible at every size, so it carries the primary lockup.
  • Display wordmark = rook-I and king-I. The I in ROOKIE is a rook, the I in KING is a king. It's the richest of the four drafts, but the cut-in pieces get fine at small sizes, so it's kept for heroes, posters and splash screens at 320 px and up.
  • Alternate = rook + king. Heavier shading and a sketchier seam (it was a draft). It's used as a large illustration, never as the logo.
  • Game pieces. Each half of the mark mirrored on its own axis gives a full Rookie and a full King. The board pieces are his shapes too.

Cool = cold, waiting, status

Steel and Mist carry the cold message, the pending invite, labels and clocks. Blue carries the action.

Warm = the win

Win Amber is the complement of his blue, so a small amount glows hard against Navy (8.3:1). It's used only for the handshake, the connection and the level-up, under 5% of any screen, and never in the logo.

Contrast (WCAG 2.x, measured): White on Navy 17.9 · White on Electric Blue 5.25 (buttons pass AA) · Electric Blue on Navy 3.4 (large type and UI only; links use Sky, 5.4) · Steel on Navy 6.3 · Amber on Navy 8.3 · Navy on Paper 16.6 · Electric Blue on Paper 4.9.

Voice and language

Taglines

  1. Play the game. Build the network. primary
  2. Every king was a rookie.
  3. One game. One real connection.
  4. Skip the cold message. Play.
  5. Make your move.

Tone rules

  • Professional first, game second. A sharp operator who plays chess, not a gamer.
  • Short and declarative. Verbs first.
  • One chess word per line at most (move, board, opening, endgame).
  • Respect rank, then remove it. Never mock rookies, never flatter kings.
  • Real results only. No invented members, stats or reviews.
  • Cold words stay cool (waiting, no reply). Warm words are for the win (connected, good game).
  • No cartoon voice, no exclamation stacks, no emoji in product UI.

Board spec

  • Light squares #1C3A8C, dark #0A1A4E, frame Night #030A24 with an Ice hairline.
  • Move hint: Sky ring and dot. Last move: Electric Blue at 22%.
  • Trail: Electric Blue to Ice, nodes on every square passed.
  • Connection: the trail turns Win Amber and the infinity loop draws between the two pieces.
  • Pieces: his Rookie (blue) and King (white), soft ground shadow, lift 5 px on hover.

Cards and surfaces

  • Surface #0B1D55, radius 16, 1 px line at Ice 14%.
  • Kicker label: Inter 600 caps in Steel, with the split chip (blue | white).
  • Win state: amber border at 55% plus a soft amber glow, never an amber fill on cards.
  • Light sections: Paper #F4F6FB, Navy text, Amber Deep for warm accents.
