The Concept

Your guardian circle
goes off-road

SH✡MER was built as a civic safety network — a community that watches over each other in real time. SH✡MER Nature extends that same guardian philosophy to the places where cell towers don't reach.

Your hiking group becomes a mesh network. Your family becomes your base camp. Emergency services are a single button away — even without signal.

  • Bluetooth + GPS mesh keeps the group connected with zero cellular coverage
  • Family portal shows real-time trail position from any browser
  • Satellite-ready SOS transmits precise GPS coordinates within seconds
  • Automated check-ins notify family at every waypoint you pass
  • Offline survival field guide: first aid, snake ID, water sourcing

Emergency Protocol

When every second counts

One button press triggers a coordinated emergency response — even without any cellular signal.

🆘

SOS Triggered

User holds the SOS button for 3 seconds. The app immediately captures current GPS coordinates, battery level, last heading, group member positions, and device sensors.

T + 0 seconds
📡

Mesh Network Broadcast

The SOS signal propagates instantly through the Bluetooth mesh. Every group member's device receives the alert, begins location logging, and switches to emergency beacon mode to assist pinpointing.

T + 3 seconds
📲

Family Alert Dispatched

Emergency contacts receive a push notification with a live GPS map pin, trail name, last photo taken, group size, and a direct link to the real-time tracking view — no app required on their end.

T + 8 seconds
🛸

Satellite Fallback Engaged

If no cellular coverage exists, SH✡MER Nature engages satellite messaging integration (Apple Emergency SOS / Garmin InReach planned) to transmit the incident package directly to rescue coordination.

T + 15 seconds
🚁

Rescue Coordination Package

Search and rescue services receive a full incident package: GPS trail history, group composition, last 30 minutes of position data, pre-filled medical information, and the optimal approach route to the incident site.

T + 2 minutes

The Israel National Trail stretches 1,000km from Rosh HaNikra in the north to the Red Sea at Eilat — passing through Mediterranean forest, Galilee mountains, Judean Hills, the Negev desert, and the Arava valley.
