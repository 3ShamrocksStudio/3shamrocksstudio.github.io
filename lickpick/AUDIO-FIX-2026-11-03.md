# LickPick audio fix, 3 Nov 2026 (lp-2026-11-03a, commit 27b3b64)

Dave's report: "LickPick still messes up the audio of uploaded videos."

## Root causes (measured, not guessed)

1. **Every Pick was silently time-stretched.** `tightAuto()` turned "Lock my playing to the beat" on by itself for any Pick with a steady beat whose notes were within 120 ms of the grid and needed up to 50% local stretch. It did this even with the band off. Saving or previewing that Pick then ran WSOLA time-warping over the player's audio. On real clips the warp moved the audio by up to ±40 ms inside the Pick, and correlation with the original fell to 0.14–0.22. That is the smeared, warbly, "damaged" sound.
2. **The preview of a locked, looped, patched or tuned Pick played the sound copy, not the video.** `planBuffer()` went through `soundBuf()`, which used `c.proxy` first. The proxy is the small 22.05 kHz, 9 kHz low-passed, mono, 40 kbps Opus sound copy kept for after a restart. Because of cause 1, nearly every Pick preview was dull and mono.
3. **A/V sync slipped by 0.2 s on Picks starting in the first 0.5 s.** The export's guess about sample timestamps (clip time or output time) was wrong for `s < 0.5`, so the sound ran `s` seconds late. The overlay (chords and tab) had the same guess.
4. **Decoders trusted the container's sample rate.** `decodeRange()` and `decodeWin()` placed decoded audio at `track.sampleRate`. When the decoder outputs a different rate (HE-AAC with SBR, or an Opus header written with the input rate), pitch, speed and length would be wrong. Each chunk is now placed at its real decoded rate. This is defensive: none of the test files had the mismatch.
5. **The last clip's band kept playing.** `openStudio()` stopped plain playback but not the band engine, so moving to another clip could leave the old band sounding.

## What changed (lickpick/app/index.html)
- `tightAuto()` now always returns false. Lock is only on when the player turns it on, and "Fix all" counts as the player turning it on.
- `planBuffer()` decodes each part from the video itself, at full rate and in stereo. It falls back to the sound copy only when the video is gone.
- `soundBuf()` prefers the real file (up to 160 MB) over the sound copy.
- `decodeRange()` and `decodeWin()` resample per chunk when the decoded rate differs from the header.
- In the export audio process and the overlay composer, the timestamp base is now whichever of clip time and output time the first sample is nearer to.
- `openStudio()` calls `stopBand()`. The player also sets `webkitPreservesPitch` for ¾× and ½× playback on Safari.
- LP_VER and the service-worker cache are both `lp-2026-11-03a`.

## Evidence (Chrome 156, 390x844 mobile, real app flow: import → scan → Pick → band → save)
| Clip | Before: preview corr / plain-save corr | After: preview corr, SNR / plain-save corr |
|---|---|---|
| r1 Android MP4 (AAC 48k) | 0.21 / 0.14 (warp ±40 ms, mono) | 1.000, 38.8 dB / 1.000 (lossless copy) |
| r4 portrait MP4 (AAC 44.1k) | 0.16 / 0.22 | 1.000, 52.7 dB / 1.000 |
| r2 iPhone-style MOV | n/a | 1.000, 33.3 dB / 1.000 |

Format matrix, synthetic take with a white flash on every beat. Files: MP4 AAC 44.1k stereo, MP4 AAC 48k mono, MOV, WebM VP8/Opus, WebM VP9/Opus mono, VFR MP4, audio-delayed MP4, fragmented recorder MP4, live WebM, AAC 22k mono.
- Preview decode: lag 0–1 ms, drift 0, no channel loss.
- Exports: lag ≤ 1 ms, A/V offset −10 ms, which is the expected frame quantisation at 30 fps. No clipping, 48 kHz stereo out.
- Picks starting at 0.2 s: 200 ms late before the fix, 0.7 ms after.
- Mastering on real Picks stays transparent: correlation 0.995 or better, clean lift of 2–6 dB, at most 3 dB of limiting, and the hush gate did not fire.

Test tools (outside the repo): scratchpad `lp/` — `t1.mjs` (format matrix), `t2.mjs` (app end to end), `an.py`, `an2.py`.
