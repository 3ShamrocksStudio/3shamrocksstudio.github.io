How to delete:

  1. Email lickpick.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my LickPick account".
  2. Send it from the Google email you signed in with, so we can find your account.
  3. We delete your account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in record and the account copy we keep: your name and email from Google, your profile, the preferences LickPick learned (taste), your streak, project settings and your "My licks" list.

What we keep

Nothing from your account. If you bought Pro, the reseller who handled the payment keeps its own receipt records as the law requires.

Videos

Your videos were never uploaded. They stay on your phone; uninstalling LickPick or clearing its data removes LickPick's copies there.
