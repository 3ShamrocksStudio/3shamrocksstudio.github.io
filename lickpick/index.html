FAQ

Good questions.

Does it work on iPhone?

LickPick is built for Android phones in Chrome, and it also runs in Chrome on a desktop. iPhone support is on the list.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. The listening, cutting and exporting all happen on your phone. Captions and the backing band are made on your phone too. None of your clips or notes are sent anywhere.

Will it delete my clips?

Never. LickPick saves new files and gives you a cleanup list. You decide what to delete from your gallery.

How does it pick "the best" part?

It tracks your beat and bars, scores every phrase for clean, confident playing and energy, and avoids stops, wrong notes and talking. You get the top phrase plus two runners-up, and you can nudge the start and end a bar at a time. Tap "Why this part?" to see a strength map of the whole take and hear every part it passed over.

What instruments does it understand?

Guitar, bass, keys and voice work best. Any clip with music in it gets scanned; a clip with no music at all is suggested for the trash.

How long, how many?

Up to 30 videos each time you add, and no limit on how many you keep in the list. Each video can be 5 seconds to 30 minutes (best under 20). A Pick needs about 20 seconds of clean playing in a row. Same limits on Free and Pro, because everything runs on your phone.

How do I record for the best Pick?

Lean the phone 1 to 2 m away with the mic clear. Start recording 5 seconds before you play and let it ring 3 seconds after. Play in a quiet room with no music in the background, keep a metronome in your headphones, and frame yourself in the middle for vertical shorts.

What do I get with Pro?

A band on every save, mixed your way. The full studio sound: Warm, Bright and Live room on top of the free Clean, plus Pro EQ (your own 5-band EQ, compressor, low cut and room, with a live spectrum and A/B). Hearing it is free; Pro saves it. Save every pick or clean cut in one tap, or each part as its own file. And every add-on we ship next (Beat FX, 90s looks, the best-licks reel) at no extra cost.
