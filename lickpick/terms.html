Using LickPick

LickPick runs on your phone and works on videos you choose. You keep all rights to your music and videos; we never receive them. Only post music you have the right to post.

Pro

Pro is a subscription that unlocks the Pro features on your phones while it is active, including Pro features and Backstage drops we add later. It renews by itself each month or year until you cancel. You can cancel any time from the link in your receipt email; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then the key stops working. Pro is for one player. Each key works on a limited number of phones; if you change phones and hit the limit, reply to your receipt email and we'll free it up.

Pro costs $2.99 a month or $14.99 a year. Prices are in US dollars; local sales tax or VAT is added at checkout where required.

Refunds

Within 14 days of buying, reply to your receipt email (or use the reseller's order page) and ask for a refund. You don't need to give a reason. After a refund, the key stops working the next time LickPick checks it.

Free users get 10 Pro saves to try Pro. During the beta, features and prices may change; we'll never take away Pro features you paid for.

No warranty
