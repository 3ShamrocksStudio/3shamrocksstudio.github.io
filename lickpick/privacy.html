Privacy

Updated 11 October 2026 (1.14) · LickPick is made by 3Shamrocks Studio.

Short version: your videos, your edits and what LickPick learns about your taste stay on your phone. We don't have accounts, servers that receive your clips, ads or trackers.

Your videos

LickPick reads the videos you add, on your phone. It keeps its own copy in the app's private storage so they're still there after a restart, together with a small sound copy and the shorts you make. Nothing is uploaded. Your gallery files are never changed or deleted. Removing a take from LickPick deletes LickPick's copy.

Your projects and taste

Picks, edits, sound settings, ratings and the preferences LickPick learns from them are stored in your browser's storage on this phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes them. "Back up" in Your taste saves a file to your own Downloads.

Listening for instruments

To keep the backing band from doubling what's already in your take, LickPick listens for drums, bass and other instruments. This runs on your phone. The first time, it downloads a small listening model (about 10 MB) from the LickPick website; your audio is never sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your phone's share sheet sends the file to the app you choose (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp…). That app's own privacy policy applies from there.

Test reports

The test report in the menu is built on your phone and only leaves it if you copy and send it yourself. It contains your device type, scan times and the names of your clips.

Pro keys and payments

LickPick never sees your card. If you buy Pro, checkout is run by our reseller (merchant of record), who takes the payment, handles tax and refunds, and emails you a licence key. Their privacy policy covers your payment details.

When you paste that key, LickPick sends the key and a device label (for example "LickPick · Android · date") to the reseller's licence service to confirm it, then re-checks about once a week when you're online. Nothing else is sent: no clips, no names of clips, no usage. Founding-tester keys are checked on the phone only.
