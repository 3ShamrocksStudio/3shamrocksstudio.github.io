The problem

For every pet parent who worries

You love your pet unconditionally. But keeping up with health, meds, vet visits, nutrition, and activity — scattered across sticky notes, reminders, and your best guess? That's a full-time job.

MILO takes that weight off your shoulders.

💊
Missed medications "Did Bella get her flea treatment this month? I think I did it last week... or was that the month before?"
🏥
Vet visits without history "What was she eating before she got sick? I wish I'd written down when the symptoms started."
📋
Records scattered everywhere "The vet card is somewhere in a drawer, vaccine records on my phone, microchip number on a piece of paper..."
Core features

One app for everything your pet needs

MILO is proactive, organized, and always ready — so you can focus on the moments that matter.

📔
Pet Health Journal

Log daily meals, water intake, mood, energy, and symptoms. Spot patterns before they become problems.

🏥
Vet Ready

Auto-generate a pre-visit health summary with symptom timeline. Show up to every appointment prepared — no guessing.

⏰
Smart Reminders

Never miss medications, vaccinations, flea treatments, or grooming. MILO tracks the schedule so you don't have to.

🏃
Activity Tracker

Log walks, play sessions, and calories burned. Connect with wearables for real-time activity data and fitness goals by breed.

🗺️
Community

Find nearby dog parks, off-leash areas, pet-friendly spots, and local meetups. Connect with pet families in your neighborhood.

🪪
Pet ID Passport

Digital passport with complete medical history. Essential for emergencies, boarding, and international travel.

🔬
Breed Insights

Behavior tips, breed-specific health risks, and nutritional advice tailored to your dog or cat — not generic advice for every pet.

📸
Memories

A photo and video timeline of your pet's milestones — from their first day home to every big adventure in between.

Killer feature

Show up to the vet prepared

MILO generates a complete pre-visit health report automatically — symptom timeline, recent meals, behavior changes, medications, and weight history — everything your vet needs before you even say hello.
