Privacy Policy

Draft version 0.1 · 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Trashure-Hunters app and its test builds

Draft for review. This page is not public yet. It describes exactly what the current app code does. Two points marked “server update” are written in the code and tested, and take effect on our test server once that update is switched on.

Trashure-Hunters is a game for children aged 8 to 14, played together with a parent or guardian. Kids clean up real litter, take a photo of it, and earn points in the game. We collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and we never show ads to children.

The short version

  • A parent creates the account. Children never get their own email or password.
  • A parent says yes first. The parent ticks a consent box before the child can play.
  • Nicknames only. We never ask for a child's real name, age, birthday, school or photo of their face.
  • No ads, no selling. The app has no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools, and we do not sell or rent any data.
  • Cleanup photos are deleted as soon as the cleanup has been checked.
  • No exact locations shown to others. Other players only see a rough area of about 1 km (server update).

Who we are

Trashure-Hunters is made by 3Shamrocks Studio (David Markovich), Israel. For any privacy question or request, email dave@3shamrocks.com.

Parental consent

Only an adult can create an account. Before a child profile can play, the parent reads a short notice and ticks “I am this child's parent or legal guardian, and I consent to my child playing Trashure-Hunters under my account.” The play button stays off until the box is ticked. The consent is saved with its version and time.

We are reviewing whether an additional verification step is needed for full compliance with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) before the app is offered to the public.

Who can see what

  • The parent can see everything about their own children's play. No other family can read it; this is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app.
  • Other players can see the shared Grime Zones on the map (their team colour and size, never who cleaned them) and the leaderboard.
  • Map: another family's Grime Zone is shown only as a rough area, snapped to a grid of about 1 km. Your own family's zones are shown at their real spot, on your own screen only. The “you are here” dot is never sent to other players. (server update)
  • Leaderboard: other players appear under a generated handle such as “Brave Ranger 27”, never under the nickname the parent typed. Your child sees their own nickname next to their own row only. (server update)

Photos

Cleanup photos go to private storage that only the parent's own account can upload to. After the cleanup check finishes, the photo is deleted automatically. Today the check is automatic and approves every photo; no person and no outside company looks at it. If we later switch on an AI check, the photo would be sent to that AI service only for the check, and we will update this page before doing so.

Please photograph the litter, not people.

No ads, no selling, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking tools and no social login. We never sell, rent or trade personal information, and we never use it for marketing to children.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, login, storage and server functions) to run the game. Map pictures come from OpenStreetMap; when the map is open, the phone downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap's servers, which see the phone's internet address like any website would, but not who the player is. The web test version is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Cleanup photos: deleted as soon as they have been checked.
  • Account and game data: kept while the account exists.
  • Grime Zones are shared parts of the game map. They hold no name or account, so they may stay after an account is deleted.

To delete a child profile or the whole account, email dave@3shamrocks.com from the parent's sign-in address. We will confirm and delete within 30 days. Deleting the parent account removes every child profile and all of their game data. A parent can also ask to see the data we hold about their child, or to correct it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Every private table is protected by row-level rules in the database, rewards are calculated on the server, and no secret keys are built into the app.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and for any important change we will tell parents in the app or by email before it takes effect.
