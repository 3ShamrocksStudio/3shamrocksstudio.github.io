The basics

What's a lick?

A lick is a short musical phrase — the kind of riff that sticks with you. A Hendrix bend. A Jaco Pastorius bass groove. A Coltrane run over a ii-V. These 5–30 second snippets are how musicians talk, learn, and trade ideas. LickPick turns them into shareable, jammable content for anyone with an instrument and a phone.

🎸
Guitar lick
A pentatonic blues bend in E minor — eight seconds of pure expression that tells you everything about someone's feel and timing.
🎵
Bass groove
A slap-and-pop figure in the pocket — rhythmically locked in, the kind you learn by ear before you ever look at the notation.
🥁
Drum fill
Two bars of paradiddle variation that flip a straight 4/4 inside out. In under ten seconds it shows you someone's whole vocabulary.
