The Problem

Your neighborhood deserves a real guardian.

WhatsApp groups are noisy. NextDoor is social, not operational. And 911 isn't designed for community-level coordination. For communities with real safety responsibilities — Shomrim organizations, HOAs, gated neighborhoods — there's been no purpose-built tool.

WhatsApp Groups

Unverified panic, missed messages, spam, and zero accountability. When something real happens, the noise-to-signal ratio makes them dangerous to rely on.

General Neighborhood Apps

Too broad, too slow, too many lost cats and political posts. These platforms weren't designed around the verified trust and speed that community security demands.

No Operational Coordination

Shomrim teams, HOA patrols, and neighborhood watches operate with no shared real-time awareness. Coordination still happens by phone call and gut instinct.

What SH✡MER Does

Every layer of your community, protected.

Seven interconnected features that give your neighborhood real situational awareness — in real time, from verified sources, with no noise.

Real-time

Verified Alerts

Neighbors report incidents from the app. Each alert passes through the trust network — nearby verified members confirm or dismiss it — before reaching the wider community. False alarms are stopped before they spread.

Guardian Layer

שומרים Layer

A live map layer showing verified guardians on patrol — their coverage zones, real-time location, and on/off-duty status. Every dot on the map is a real person who has been through community verification.

Perimeter Layer

גבול Border Zone

Draw and manage your community's boundary directly on the map. Every alert is automatically tagged by its position relative to the perimeter — inside, at the edge, or approaching from outside.

Command Center

Dashfeed

The admin-side monitoring dashboard. Live presence dots, incident feeds, escalation queues, sector coverage breakdowns — everything a safety coordinator needs to manage an active situation in one view.

Emergency

One-Tap Escalation

When seconds count, one button sends an emergency alert to every verified member simultaneously — no typing, no menu navigation. Your entire network gets the alert with location coordinates in under three seconds.

Trust System

Verified Trust Circles

Invite-only access with multi-layer identity verification. New members are vouched for by existing members in your community. No anonymous accounts, no outsider access — just the people you actually trust.

How It Works

Three taps. Full community coverage.

From a neighbor spotting something to your entire verified network being notified — the whole loop runs in under two minutes, with anti-spam verification built in.

01

Report

A community member spots something suspicious. They open SH✡MER and submit a report — with photo, precise location pin, and incident category. Takes under 15 seconds.

02

Verify

Nearby verified guardians receive the unconfirmed alert. Three confirmations locks it as real. Dismissed by two? It's silently dropped. No noise reaches the community feed.

03

Alert Network

A verified alert pushes instantly to every community member — with map coordinates, incident photo, and category. The full network knows in seconds. Guardians can respond directly from the notification.

Command Center

Admin visibility, in real time.

The Dashfeed is the operations layer for community safety coordinators. Every active guardian, every live alert, every sector status — in a single monitoring view that feels like a war room, not a chat app.

Live Presence Map

See which guardians are active, their last known position, and patrol coverage zones — all updating in real time as people move.

Multi-org Isolation

Each community operates its own private bubble. Admins control their own membership, perimeters, and escalation protocols — completely separate from other orgs on the platform.

Full Incident Timeline

Every report, verification, escalation, and resolution is logged with timestamps and member IDs — for post-incident review, accountability, and community reporting.

Under the Hood

Built for performance, privacy, and scale.

SH✡MER is a production-grade mobile platform — not a prototype. Every architecture decision is chosen for real-time reliability, community-level data isolation, and sub-second alert propagation.

Real-time

Firebase Realtime Database

All live map data, alert state, and guardian presence runs through Firebase RTDB. Propagation is sub-100ms — every connected client sees a guardian's position update the instant it changes, with no polling.

Auth & Data

Supabase with Row-Level Security

Phone-number verification and organization-scoped access through Supabase Auth. Row-level security enforces community isolation at the database layer — no application-level guard needed. No org can see another's data.

Mobile

React Native + Expo EAS

A single shared codebase for iOS and Android. Expo EAS manages over-the-air updates and native push notifications — critical patches deploy without app store review delays. Background location tracking with battery efficiency.

Maps

Leaflet.js — Custom Tri-Layer

Three independently toggleable map layers — Shomrim guardian positions, community perimeter (גבול), and live alert pins — built on custom Leaflet.js. Vector rendering for crisp display at any zoom level.
