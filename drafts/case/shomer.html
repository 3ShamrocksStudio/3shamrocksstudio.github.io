The problem

Whoever is closest gets there first.

In danger, help is often already nearby.

A neighbour, a passer-by, a friend two streets away. They just don’t know.

Local authorities need to see what happens on their ground, without exposing anyone.

What I did

One person. One app, one board.

01

One press SOS.

A loud alarm. An alert to the people you chose and to users nearby.

They see the fastest route to you.

02

Guardian circles.

Family, friends, neighbours, in groups you set.

Your SOS reaches them even when they are far away.

03

SH✡MER Command for local authorities.

Live SOS calls on a map of the authority’s area. Response steps, an incident log, a proper close.

Data is aggregated and blurred. No names, no phones, no exact location.
